# Editto Nei giorni · English editorial summary

## Summary

At the beginning of his pontificate, Pius IX grants clemency to subjects punished or exiled for political offenses. He describes concern for their families and for young people whom he regards as misled by political agitation. Prisoners may receive remission of their remaining sentences, and political exiles may seek return within a year, on a written pledge to honor the grant and fulfill the duties of loyal subjects.

The edict also removes specified political restrictions and terminates unfinished prosecutions for purely political offenses, while allowing an accused person to request continuation to establish innocence. It reserves separate treatment for implicated clergy, military officers, and government employees, and excludes ordinary crimes. Pius hopes forgiveness will heal civil hatred, but states that justice remains a sovereign’s primary duty if that confidence is abused.

## Key themes

- Conditional clemency for political prisoners and exiles
- Termination of prosecutions and restoration of specified civic capacities
- Exclusions for ordinary crimes and reserved official cases
