# Ecclesia Christi · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VII ratifies the agreement negotiated with the French Republic to restore Catholic worship and ecclesial unity after revolutionary disruption. He recounts the negotiations, consultation with cardinals, and Cardinal Consalvi's mission, explaining that extraordinary concessions were warranted by the needs of peace and religion. Catholicism is recognized as the religion of the great majority of French citizens, and its public exercise is restored subject to government regulations for public tranquility.

The constitution sets out the settlement's principal terms. Dioceses will be redrawn by agreement; existing bishops will be asked to resign; the First Consul will nominate bishops, with canonical institution reserved to the pope. Bishops and clergy must take the stipulated loyalty oath, and public prayers will include the Republic and consuls. Bishops will appoint qualified parish priests acceptable to the government, establish parish boundaries with its consent, and maintain chapters and seminaries. Unsold churches needed for worship will be made available, purchasers of alienated ecclesiastical property will be left undisturbed, and the state will support bishops and parish priests. Pius confirms the agreement, directs its peaceful observance, and notes separate provision for clergy who had married or abandoned ministry.

## Key themes

- Restoration of public Catholic worship and ecclesial unity
- Shared procedures for dioceses and episcopal appointments
- Clerical loyalty, public order, and government consent
- Property settlement and financial support for the clergy
