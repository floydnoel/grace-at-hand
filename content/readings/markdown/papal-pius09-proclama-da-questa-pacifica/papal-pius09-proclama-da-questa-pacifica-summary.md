# Proclama Da questa pacifica · English editorial summary

## Summary

Writing from Gaeta, Pius IX rejects the decree calling a national assembly to determine a new political order for the Papal States. He says that invitations to return to Rome have offered neither condemnation of the violence against him and his household nor adequate guarantees of security. He regards the proposed assembly as a further attempt to overthrow a sovereignty that he is bound to preserve.

The proclamation prohibits subjects from participating in meetings to elect its representatives and declares that those responsible for attacks on papal temporal authority have incurred ecclesiastical censures, particularly major excommunication. Pius presents these measures as a duty toward the Church’s patrimony, while insisting that severity must be accompanied by mercy. He prays for the repentance of those involved, expresses willingness to endure persecution, and looks forward to welcoming them back into communion.

## Key themes

- Rejection of an assembly claiming power over papal government
- Prohibition of electoral participation and declaration of censures
- Defense of sovereignty accompanied by prayer for repentance
