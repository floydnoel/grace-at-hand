# Ubi urbaniano · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX states that reliable testimony has confirmed reports of severe Russian measures against Catholics following the Polish uprising. He describes broken agreements, restrictions on preaching and communication with Rome, interference with religious adherence, suppression of monasteries, confiscations, exile, imprisonment, and violence against clergy and faithful. His concern includes Catholics of both Latin and Greek rites.

He particularly rejects the government’s purported deprivation of the exiled archbishop of Warsaw’s jurisdiction and its appointment of his vicar general as diocesan administrator. Episcopal authority, he insists, does not derive from the civil power; the archbishop remains legitimate, and the vicar must act under him. At the same time, Pius explicitly declines to approve the Polish revolt and reaffirms obedience to civil laws compatible with divine and ecclesiastical law. The encyclical warns rulers of their accountability before God and encourages bishops and faithful to persevere in religion, pastoral care, and charity despite coercion.

## Key themes

- Reported religious coercion and persecution under Russian rule
- Defense of the exiled Warsaw archbishop’s jurisdiction
- Condemnation of persecution without endorsement of the uprising
