# Quanta Laetitia · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII reorganizes Scotland's Catholic missionary government into eastern, western, and northern apostolic vicariates. He welcomes reports of substantial Catholic growth and accepts the local bishops' argument that the former division between Highland and Lowland jurisdictions no longer adequately serves the increased population. The decision follows Alexander Paterson's consultation in Rome and examination by Propaganda Fide.

The brief defines the three districts by counties, islands, and specified boundaries, with seats at Edinburgh, Glasgow, and Aberdeen. Alexander Cameron remains vicar of the eastern district, with Paterson exercising the full jurisdiction already granted to him because of Cameron's age and ill health. Ranald Macdonald is assigned the western district, and James Francis Kyle, separately named titular bishop of Germanicia, receives the northern one. Leo encourages the vicars to continue their pastoral labor and expresses hope that closer provision will support further growth of Catholic life.

## Key themes

- Three districts replacing the Highland–Lowland arrangement
- Territorial boundaries and seats of the vicariates
- Assignments of vicars and provision for an elderly bishop
