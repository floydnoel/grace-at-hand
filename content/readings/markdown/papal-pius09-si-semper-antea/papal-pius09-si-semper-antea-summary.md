# Si semper antea · English editorial summary

## Summary

In his first address to the cardinals after returning to Rome, Pius IX gives thanks for the end of more than sixteen months of exile. He acknowledges the hospitality of the king and queen of the Two Sicilies, French military intervention, Austrian and Spanish assistance, diplomatic support from other rulers, and the prayers and material generosity of Catholics. He interprets the restoration as an instance of divine providence protecting the Church.

The allocution turns from thanksgiving to continuing concerns. Pius calls bishops and clergy to united pastoral action, sound instruction, holiness, and resistance to errors. He praises Austrian measures enlarging ecclesiastical freedom, while protesting Piedmontese legislation and the imprisonment of Archbishop Luigi Fransoni of Turin. He also expresses concern for Catholic interests in Belgium and invites non-Catholics to communion with Rome. Throughout, the pope connects protection of papal temporal government with freedom for his spiritual ministry and concludes by praying for the Church, its benefactors, and peace throughout the world.

## Key themes

- Thanksgiving for restoration and international assistance
- Episcopal unity, preaching, and clerical holiness
- Contrasting assessments of ecclesiastical policy in Austria and Piedmont
