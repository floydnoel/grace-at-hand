# Ex quo infensissimi · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX praises the papal troops who resisted incursions into the remaining Papal States and defeated an attempted movement against Rome. He describes armed bands, conspirators within the city, and the danger they posed to inhabitants and sacred buildings from the perspective of his own government. The brief especially commemorates the victory of papal and auxiliary French forces near Mentana.

To preserve its memory, he institutes a silver, eight-pointed cross bearing papal emblems and inscriptions celebrating faith, valor, and victory. He permits papal soldiers and French soldiers who fought at Mentana to wear it on a specified ribbon. Papal troops also receive a reduction in the service time required for increased pay and other military benefits. The document publicly honors those who died defending papal rights and Rome, presenting their sacrifice as service to the Apostolic See and the Catholic world.

## Key themes

- Defense of the remaining Papal States and Rome
- Papal and French forces at Mentana
- Commemorative decoration and military service benefits
