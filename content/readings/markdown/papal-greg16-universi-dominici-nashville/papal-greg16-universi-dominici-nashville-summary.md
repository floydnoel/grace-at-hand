# Universi Dominici Nashville · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI establishes Nashville as a new episcopal see after a petition from the bishops assembled at Baltimore's provincial council in April 1837. Their request argues that a separate diocese for the populous city and the surrounding territory would benefit Catholic religion and the care of souls.

The brief assigns the whole of Tennessee to Nashville, detaching it from the diocese of Bardstown. A bishop is to be chosen by the Holy See and given the customary faculties for his ministry. Following consultation with Propaganda Fide, the pope gives the new see its own distinct government. The act establishes the diocese but does not name its first bishop.

## Key themes

- A new diocese requested by the Baltimore provincial council
- Tennessee assigned to Nashville’s jurisdiction
- A bishop to be appointed by the Holy See
