# Litteras accepimus · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI explains to the bishop of Rennes why Lamennais's earlier statements have not yet satisfied him that the writer accepts the pope's doctrinal judgment. The abandonment of his periodical and associated organization initially encouraged hope, but later published statements and writings connected with his followers renewed concern. Gregory distinguishes verbal respect for papal authority from actual acceptance of the teaching at issue.

The pope welcomes Lamennais's request for a clear statement of what is required. He asks for unambiguous acceptance of the doctrine set out in his encyclical and a commitment neither to write nor approve anything contrary to it. The bishop is commissioned to convey this requirement and use his influence toward reconciliation. Gregory expresses a desire to receive Lamennais with paternal affection once his submission is demonstrated in both words and conduct.

## Key themes

- Distinguishing declared deference from doctrinal agreement
- Unreserved acceptance of the encyclical and consistency in future writings
- The bishop of Rennes as mediator of reconciliation
