# Quel Dio · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI announces the restoration of order in the Papal States and thanks the Roman population, loyal provincial volunteers, civic militia, and Austrian forces for their support. He interprets the outcome as divine assistance and defends the pope's temporal sovereignty as serving freedom in the exercise of spiritual authority. He also distinguishes the leaders of rebellion from inhabitants whom he regards as coerced or frightened into participation.

The pope promises attention to genuine needs and measures for his subjects' welfare, but insists that peace requires protection against renewed disorder. He rejects an act obtained at Ancona from Cardinal Benvenuti while the cardinal was captive, arguing that coercion and loss of delegated authority made it invalid. Gregory presents the government's next steps as a balance of clemency and justice, with security of persons, morals, and property as their stated objective.

## Key themes

- Restoration of papal government and praise for loyal supporters
- Rejection of a concession obtained from a captive cardinal
- Clemency, justice, and plans for public welfare
