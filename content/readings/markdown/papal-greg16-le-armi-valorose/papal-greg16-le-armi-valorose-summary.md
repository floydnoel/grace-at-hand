# Le armi valorose · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI addresses the inhabitants of the four Legations as Austrian forces withdraw after suppressing unrest. He praises the troops' conduct and presents their intervention, requested from Emperor Francis I, as a service restoring order and protecting the papal territories. He expects the population to prevent renewed disturbances through religious fidelity, obedience, and civil concord.

The pope chooses a paternal appeal while reserving a stricter sovereign response if rebellion returns. He expresses a desire to put the past behind him and assures those who lost his favor that clear repentance can recover it. Mutual love should reunite the population as one family, with virtue and fidelity providing its honorable distinctions. He links stable peace with his ability to pursue measures for the subjects' welfare.

## Key themes

- Withdrawal of Austrian forces and expectations of public order
- Paternal reconciliation conditioned on repentance
- Civil concord as the basis for public welfare
