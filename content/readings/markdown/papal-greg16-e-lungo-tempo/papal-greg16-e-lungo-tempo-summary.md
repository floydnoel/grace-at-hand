# È lungo tempo · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI welcomes a friendly letter and envoys from Ubie, ruler of Tigré in Abyssinia, expressing joy that affection has developed despite a long religious separation. He presents the pope's ministry as a spiritual resource intended for the ruler and his people, whose salvation is the object of his concern.

The exchange of representatives and gifts becomes an occasion to invite deeper unity. Gregory notes that people from Ubie's country remain with him while his own spiritual sons are with the ruler. He asks Ubie to consider how their friendship could become enduring through union in the faith that the pope identifies as the foundation of true charity.

## Key themes

- Friendly exchange of envoys and gifts
- An invitation to religious unity grounded in charity
