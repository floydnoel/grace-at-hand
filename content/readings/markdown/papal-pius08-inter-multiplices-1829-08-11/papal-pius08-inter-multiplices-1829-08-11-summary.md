# Inter multiplices · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VIII separates Prince Edward Island, New Brunswick, and the Magdalen Islands from Quebec and constitutes them as a distinct diocese named for its episcopal seat, Charlottetown. The decision follows examination by Propaganda Fide and the express consent of Archbishop Bernard-Claude Panet of Quebec.

The pope presents the new arrangement as a way to make pastoral administration more effective across these regions. The diocese is placed immediately under the Holy See until the Holy See determines otherwise. The brief permanently effects the territorial separation and overrides contrary privileges, customs, and obligations connected with Quebec insofar as they conflict with the new organization.

## Key themes

- Creation of the Diocese of Charlottetown
- Separation of specified Canadian territories from Quebec
- Immediate dependence on the Holy See
