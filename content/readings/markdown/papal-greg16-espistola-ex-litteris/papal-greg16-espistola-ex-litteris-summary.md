# Espistola Ex litteris · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI answers the archbishop of Paris's request for his judgment on a proposed organization for Catholic missions. He confirms that the Holy See knew of the initiative from its beginning, referring to an accompanying letter from Cardinal Franzoni of Propaganda Fide. Nevertheless, for weighty circumstances that this letter does not specify, the pope has decided that the project should not proceed.

Gregory explicitly praises the religious zeal and good intentions of those prepared to support it. He asks the archbishop to use prudence, pastoral influence, and persuasive authority to secure acceptance of the Holy See's decision. Further practical guidance is left to the cardinal's accompanying communication.

## Key themes

- Consultation about a proposed missionary organization
- Praise for its supporters alongside refusal of the project
- Episcopal responsibility for communicating the decision
