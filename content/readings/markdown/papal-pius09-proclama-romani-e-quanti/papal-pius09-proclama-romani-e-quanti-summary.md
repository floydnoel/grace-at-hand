# Proclama Romani, e quanti · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX appeals to Romans and other papal subjects to respect the Church’s ministers and religious institutions. Warning against violence and scandal, he asks them to bring justified complaints about misconduct through lawful representations, which he promises to consider as supreme pontiff.

He hopes his paternal admonition will prevent attacks, but states that the civic guard and other forces responsible for public order will act if necessary. The proclamation calls for calm and practical religious conduct throughout the Papal States. It ends with an apostolic blessing and the hope that restraint will avert harsher measures and the divine punishments he associates with ingratitude.

## Key themes

- Protection of clergy and religious institutions from attack
- Lawful complaints concerning ecclesiastical misconduct
- Appeal for calm backed by public-order authorities
