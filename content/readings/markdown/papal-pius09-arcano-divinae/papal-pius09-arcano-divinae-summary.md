# Arcano divinae · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX addresses bishops of Eastern churches outside communion with the Apostolic See, inviting them to the general council scheduled to open in Rome on 8 December of the following year. He presents his pastoral concern as extending to all baptized Christians and recalls his earlier appeal to the East, acknowledging that it did not obtain the result he hoped for.

The letter asks these bishops to attend in the manner of their predecessors at the councils of Lyon and Florence, with the aim of restoring ecclesial communion. It invokes Eastern Fathers and the shared Christian desire for peace, framing reunion with Rome as the recovery of an ancient bond. Pius prays that division will give way to a common profession of faith and praise of God. Its invitation is inseparable from the pope’s understanding of the Apostolic See as the center of Catholic truth and unity.

## Key themes

- Invitation to Eastern bishops outside Roman communion
- Appeal to earlier councils and Eastern Christian tradition
- Prayer for restored communion and Christian unity
