# Non sine gravissimo · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX directs his apostolic delegate to return to Constantinople and address conflict among Armenian Catholics. He condemns opposition to Patriarch Anthony Peter IX and his vicar, challenges to the patriarch’s election, the omission of his liturgical commemoration, and the establishment of a self-described independent Armenian Catholic church. He praises those who remained obedient and asks the delegate to restore order and strengthen faith.

The letter distinguishes the preservation of Eastern rites from the regulation of ecclesiastical discipline. Pius affirms the retention of compatible Eastern liturgical traditions while insisting that they do not exempt Church government from papal authority or essential canonical norms. He rejects an arrangement placing opponents directly under the delegate as a means of bypassing their patriarch. While affirming the right of appeal to Rome, he warns against using appeals to sustain disobedience. Further instructions concern clergy, monks, and lay involvement in church affairs, expressing the hierarchical discipline he seeks to enforce in this dispute.

## Key themes

- Defense of the recognized patriarch and his vicar
- Distinction between Eastern rites and canonical discipline
- Legitimate appeals distinguished from evasion of episcopal authority
