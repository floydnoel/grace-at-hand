# Ex aliis Nostris · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX grants a jubilee indulgence to accompany the public prayers requested in another encyclical issued the same day. Each bishop is to designate a month during which the faithful may complete confession, Communion, visits to selected churches, fasting, almsgiving, and support for the Propagation of the Faith. The appointed prayers concern the Church, the Apostolic See, Christian unity, and peace among rulers. The indulgence may also be applied in suffrage for the departed.

The letter makes accommodations for the cloistered, imprisoned, sick, and others unable to perform particular works. It authorizes specially selected confessors to exercise extensive but temporary faculties regarding reserved sins, censures, vows, and some canonical irregularities. These powers have explicit exceptions, including protections concerning third parties and existing rules for abuses connected with confession. Those under personally imposed censures remain subject to obligations of satisfaction. Bishops are responsible for the local timetable and the selection of suitable confessors.

## Key themes

- A diocesan month of sacramental and penitential observances
- Charity to the poor and support for missions
- Exceptional confessional faculties with explicit exclusions
