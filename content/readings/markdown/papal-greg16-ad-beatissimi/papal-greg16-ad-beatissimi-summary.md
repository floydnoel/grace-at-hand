# Ad beatissimi · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI extends the devotional privileges associated with the underground altar above the tomb of Peter and Paul in the Vatican basilica. Following earlier popes' efforts to honor the apostles through the basilica's adornment and spiritual privileges, he declares this altar perpetually privileged for the living and the dead. The brief permits priests to celebrate the apostles' votive Mass there throughout the year, with specified major liturgical exceptions, and makes a further provision for the octave of their feast.

The document also grants a monthly plenary indulgence to visitors who fulfill its sacramental and prayer requirements, together with a lesser indulgence for other devout visits. These historical grants may be applied in suffrage for the departed. The prescribed prayers concern the well-being of the Roman Church and the spread of the Catholic faith.

## Key themes

- Devotion to Peter and Paul at their Vatican tomb
- Historical privileges for Mass at the underground altar
- Indulgences, sacramental preparation, and prayer for the departed
