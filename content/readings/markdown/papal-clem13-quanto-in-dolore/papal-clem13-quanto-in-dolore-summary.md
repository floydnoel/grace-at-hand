# Quanto in dolore · English editorial summary

## Summary

Clement XIII describes what he regards as a severe attack on ecclesiastical authority in France. He complains that defenders of papal and conciliar decisions are silenced while opponents circulate their views freely; that clergy following ecclesiastical discipline face imprisonment, exile, and public disgrace; and that bishops are being bypassed in theological teaching and the education of youth.

He gives particular attention to the Society of Jesus, defending its ecclesiastical approval and pastoral usefulness against its opponents’ accusations. He rejects secular judgments nullifying religious vows, a matter he reserves to the Church. Addressing the assembled French clergy, Clement asks the bishops to discuss these common dangers alongside temporal affairs and, if necessary, petition the king for protection. He promises the support of his apostolic authority and prayer, calling for courage and perseverance in their pastoral responsibilities. The letter’s descriptions of opponents and their motives express Clement’s own assessment of the conflict.

## Key themes

- Restrictions on clergy and episcopal oversight of education
- Papal defense of the Society of Jesus
- Bishops’ common action and appeal to royal protection
