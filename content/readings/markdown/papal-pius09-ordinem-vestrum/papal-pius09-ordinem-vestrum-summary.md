# Ordinem vestrum · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX announces episcopal appointments to Italian dioceses left vacant for prolonged periods, with further appointments to follow. He describes the spiritual harm caused by the lack of pastors amid political conflict and attacks on Catholic life. The appointments, he states, exercise authority received from Christ for the salvation of souls and do not signify acceptance of the government’s proposed guarantees for the papacy, which he continues to reject.

The address then condemns opposition elsewhere in Europe to the First Vatican Council and its definitions of papal jurisdiction and infallible teaching. Pius disputes the claim that the council changed ancient doctrine or created a danger to civil government. He criticizes official support for its opponents and praises the bishops defending the council, particularly the archbishop of Munich, together with their clergy and faithful. He closes with prayer for those in error and for strength, peace, and perseverance in the Church.

## Key themes

- Pastoral necessity of filling vacant Italian bishoprics
- Appointments without acceptance of the political guarantees
- Defense of Vatican I against claims of doctrinal innovation
