# Vineam quam plantavit · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VII seeks the cooperation of French bishops and cathedral chapters in a proposed expansion and redrawing of dioceses after years of upheaval. King Louis has requested an increase in episcopal sees, and the pope presents the plan as the fulfillment of a longstanding pastoral desire that adverse circumstances had previously prevented.

The letter argues that more bishops and more suitable boundaries will improve diocesan government and the care of souls throughout France. It asks recipients to consent promptly to the proposed territorial changes so that disputes do not obstruct their implementation. Pius appeals to Christ's redemption of the faithful as the measure of the effort their pastors should be willing to undertake. The document announces and solicits support for the reorganization without itself supplying a detailed territorial schedule.

## Key themes

- Expansion of the French episcopal structure
- Diocesan boundaries adapted to pastoral needs
- Cooperation among bishops, chapters, crown, and papacy
