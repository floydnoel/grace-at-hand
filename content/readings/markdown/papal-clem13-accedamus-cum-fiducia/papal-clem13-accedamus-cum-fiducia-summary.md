# Accedamus cum fiducia · English editorial summary

## Summary

Clement XIII invites Christians to seek divine mercy through sincere repentance, prayer, fasting, and almsgiving. To encourage these practices, he proclaims a special jubilee indulgence and sets out its conditions for Rome and other places subject to the Holy See. The prescribed observances include visiting designated churches, confession, Communion, fasting, and assistance to the poor. The grant may also be applied in suffrage for the faithful departed.

The brief makes provision for cloistered religious, prisoners, the sick, and others unable to complete the usual works: an approved confessor may substitute suitable acts or allow another time. It also gives designated confessors exceptional, limited faculties to absolve reserved sins and censures and commute certain vows. Clement expressly excludes a general removal of canonical disabilities and requires satisfaction or agreement with injured parties in specified public cases. Authorized copies are to carry the same force as the original letter.

## Key themes

- Repentance expressed in prayer, fasting, and almsgiving
- Jubilee conditions and accommodations for impeded participants
- Limited confessional faculties and obligations toward injured parties
