# Quo impensiore · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX follows up his earlier intervention in the Armenian Catholic Church, describing the failure of his delegate’s warnings to restore obedience in Constantinople. He deplores clergy who continued ministry after suspension and monks who resisted apostolic visitation, while praising the faithful who remained loyal, particularly the Vienna Mekhitarists. He maintains that professions of fidelity to Rome are contradicted by persistent rejection of the recognized patriarch, his vicar, and the papal delegate.

A central distinction concerns rite and discipline. Pius denies that the dispute involves replacing Eastern rites with Latin worship. He affirms the preservation of rites compatible with Catholic faith while asserting papal authority to restore essential canonical discipline. He recalls the Holy See’s support for Armenian ecclesiastical institutions and warns the faithful against arguments he considers misleading. Further separation and sanctions are threatened if resistance continues, but he hopes repentance will spare him that necessity and permit a paternal reconciliation.

## Key themes

- Continued ministry after suspension and resistance to visitation
- Preservation of Eastern rites distinguished from canonical discipline
- Warning of further sanctions and invitation to reconciliation
