# Religiosas regularium · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX orders an apostolic visitation of the Armenian Antonian monks and appoints Archbishop Antonio Giuseppe Pluym as visitor of all their monasteries and hospices. He recounts continuing disputes with the patriarchs of Cilicia, unauthorized changes to the order’s constitutions, and the transformation of the abbot general’s limited term into a life appointment combined with episcopal dignity. Recent participation by Antonian monks in resistance to the Armenian patriarch and his vicar adds urgency to the inquiry.

The visitor receives the faculties needed to investigate and act, including authority to subdelegate suitable persons. To prevent obstruction, Placido Kasangian must relinquish the office of abbot general, which he had retained by papal indulgence despite disapproval of the underlying arrangement. All monks are required to receive the visitor, cooperate, and obey his lawful directions. Pius confirms the force of canonically imposed penalties against resistance, presenting the intervention as a remedy for religious and ecclesiastical disorder.

## Key themes

- Investigation of unauthorized constitutional and governmental changes
- Visitation of every house and power of subdelegation
- Removal of the abbot general and required cooperation
