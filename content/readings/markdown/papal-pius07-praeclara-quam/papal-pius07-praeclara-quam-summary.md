# Praeclara quam · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VII thanks four French prelates who had received special faculties in 1817 to conduct the canonical investigations required for selecting new bishops. He praises their religion, prudence, fidelity, and careful fulfillment of the commission entrusted to them by the brief Ubi primum.

With the arrival in France of Vincenzo, archbishop of Nisibis and apostolic nuncio to the French king, their exceptional assignment has ended. The brief formally acknowledges their release from that duty and commends their service. It is a concise act of gratitude and administrative transition within the restoration of regular arrangements for episcopal appointments.

## Key themes

- Canonical investigations preceding episcopal appointments
- Completion of a special delegation to French prelates
- Return of responsibility following the nuncio's arrival
