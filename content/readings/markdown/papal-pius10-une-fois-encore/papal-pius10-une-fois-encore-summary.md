## Summary

This encyclical, addressed to the French Church in 1907, condemns the French law on the separation of Church and State and the establishment of *associations cultuelles* (religious associations). Pius X defends the Church against accusations of provoking religious war, rejects the spoliation of ecclesiastical property, and refuses to accept any arrangement that violates the Church's divine constitution by subordinating the hierarchy to civil authority.

## Historical Context

Following the 1905 French Law of Separation, which unilaterally ended the Concordat of 1801 and severed ties between the French Republic and the Catholic Church, the French government introduced *associations cultuelles* — lay-run bodies that would control Church property and worship. Pius X condemned these associations in the 1906 encyclicals *Vehementer Nos* and *Gravissimo Officii*. By early 1907, the French Parliament passed further laws escalating the conflict, expelling bishops from their residences, closing seminaries, and confiscating Church property. *Une Fois Encore* (French for "Once Again") is a follow-up addressing this worsening persecution.

## Key Themes

- **Separation of Church and State** — Pius X rejects the French model of separation as a vehicle for dechristianization and the destruction of the Church's divine constitution.
- **Spoliation of Church property** — The encyclical argues that property was violently taken, not abandoned, and that the state's legal maneuvers (declaring property *sans maître*) amount to transparent sophistry and insult added to injury.
- **Defense of ecclesiastical hierarchy** — The *associations cultuelles* are condemned because they strip bishops of authority over worship and property, place lay associations outside episcopal jurisdiction, and make them answerable to civil power.
- **Calm in persecution** — Pius X praises the French clergy and laity for their unity, fidelity to the Apostolic See, and steadfastness under trial, offering them fatherly comfort and encouragement.
- **Providential hope** — Despite the violence, he expresses confidence that victory will ultimately remain with the Church and that history will vindicate his defense of the hierarchy's inviolability.

## Significance

*Une Fois Encore* is the third in a trilogy of Pius X encyclicals responding to the French Separation laws, completing the Church's principled refusal to accept a secular settlement that subordinates ecclesial governance to the state. It marks a definitive moment in the Church's modern confrontation with laïcité (militant secularism) and remains a reference point for Catholic teaching on religious liberty, the rights of the Church, and the proper relationship between spiritual and temporal power. The encyclical's insistence that the Church cannot consent to the violation of its divine constitution — even at the cost of material ruin — shaped Catholic social and political thought well into the 20th century.
