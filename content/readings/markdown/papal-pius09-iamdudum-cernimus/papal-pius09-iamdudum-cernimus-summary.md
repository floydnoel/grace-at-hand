# Iamdudum cernimus · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX responds to calls for reconciliation with progress, liberalism, and modern civilization. He distinguishes genuine cultural and moral advancement, which he claims the Church has long promoted, from a political program he believes weakens religion through broken agreements, confiscations, restrictions on Catholic education, and unequal treatment of Catholic speech. His rejection is directed at this described system and its effects on the Church.

The allocution also defends his conduct as temporal ruler. Pius recalls earlier administrative concessions and contends that revolutionary leaders sought separation from papal sovereignty rather than reform. He refuses to recognize the annexation of papal provinces, arguing that successful force cannot establish a just title to another’s property. Against demands that he relinquish these claims, he cites widespread Catholic support and the Holy See’s responsibility to uphold justice. He nevertheless distinguishes resistance to injustice from personal vengeance, praying for opponents and expressing readiness to forgive those who repent.

## Key themes

- Distinction between genuine civilization and policies hostile to the Church
- Refusal to legitimate the annexation of papal territory
- Defense of moral principle joined to prayer and forgiveness
