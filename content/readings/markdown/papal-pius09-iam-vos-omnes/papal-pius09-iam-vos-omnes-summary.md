# Iam vos omnes · English editorial summary

## Summary

On the occasion of the forthcoming ecumenical council, Pius IX appeals to Protestants and other Christians outside Catholic communion to reconsider their ecclesial position and return to unity with Rome. He explains that the council will address errors and strengthen faith, justice, and peace among Catholics, and sees its assembly as an additional sign of the Church’s unity and vitality.

Pius argues that Christ established one enduring Church on Peter, with authority to preserve revelation, teach, govern, and communicate sacramental grace. He contrasts this claim with the divisions and doctrinal changes he attributes to separated religious bodies, connecting religious fragmentation with social disorder. The letter urges prayer for the removal of division and presents the pope’s invitation as a duty of pastoral love toward all Christians.

## Key themes

- The forthcoming council as an occasion for an appeal to unity
- Petrine authority and the preservation of revealed faith
- Prayer and pastoral invitation to Christians outside Catholic communion
