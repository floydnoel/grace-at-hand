# In magnis illis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX addresses the Catholic hierarchy and faithful of the Netherlands after Hermann Heycamp consecrated Caspar Johannes Rinkel and the German Old Catholic leader Joseph Hubert Reinkens. He rejects Heycamp’s defense of these acts and his criticism of the First Vatican Council’s teaching on the Apostolic See. Recalling his earlier judgment concerning Reinkens, the pope now focuses on Rinkel’s election and consecration for Haarlem.

He declares the election void and the consecration unlawful and sacrilegious, excommunicates the consecrator, Rinkel, and those who assisted, and prohibits Rinkel from exercising episcopal functions. Pius frames the intervention as protection of ecclesial unity and the faithful against unauthorized ministry. He closes by expressing his desire to receive the separated persons back into communion and asks Catholics to pray for their repentance and for perseverance in faith.

## Key themes

- Rejection of unauthorized episcopal election and ministry
- Canonical censures directed at named persons and collaborators
- Protection of communion and prayer for reconciliation
