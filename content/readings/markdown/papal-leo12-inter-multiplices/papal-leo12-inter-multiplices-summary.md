# Inter Multiplices · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII establishes a diocese with its episcopal seat at Detroit for the territories described in the brief as Michigan and the Northwest. He acts on reports from Archbishop Ambrose Maréchal of Baltimore, the bishop of Cincinnati, and other witnesses concerning the benefit of a distinct jurisdiction for strengthening religious life.

These territories had been under the apostolic administration of Cincinnati's bishop. The pope now constitutes them as one diocese whose boundaries correspond to the designated territories and grants the customary canonical rights, honors, privileges, and obligations. The concluding provisions require observance of the new arrangement and exclude contrary interpretations. The brief concerns the erection and territorial definition of the see, without naming a bishop to occupy it.

## Key themes

- A distinct diocese for Michigan and the Northwest
- Transition from apostolic administration under Cincinnati
- Canonical rights and territorial definition of the new see
