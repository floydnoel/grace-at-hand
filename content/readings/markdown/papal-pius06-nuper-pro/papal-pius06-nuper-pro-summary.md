# Nuper pro · English editorial summary

## Summary

Responding to Queen Maria Francesca's petition, Pius VI addresses inherited Portuguese estates whose revenues scarcely suffice to discharge the pious obligations attached to them. He grants the patriarch, archbishops, and bishops of Portugal and the Algarves, acting as apostolic delegates, a special power to examine these burdens outside a synod on this occasion.

They must investigate the particular bequests and available assets carefully, then determine conscientiously what best serves God's honor and the churches' benefit. The brief gives their resulting arrangements legal force and derogates from contrary testamentary provisions and ecclesiastical regulations insofar as necessary. It establishes a specific remedy for unsustainable obligations rather than an unrestricted release from donors' intentions.

## Key themes

- Pious endowments exceeding the resources of inherited estates
- Special episcopal delegation after a royal petition
- Careful investigation and adjustment of testamentary obligations
