# Novos et ante · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX protests the Sardinian army’s invasion of further papal provinces after the earlier seizure of Emilia. He rejects the demand that foreign soldiers in papal service be dismissed, defending both a government’s right to recruit abroad and the religious motives of Catholic volunteers. He recounts the papal commander’s attempted withdrawal toward Ancona, the ensuing battle against superior forces, and the deaths of soldiers whose fidelity he praises and whose families he seeks to console.

The allocution declares the occupation and its associated acts unlawful and void. Pius connects territorial independence with the pope’s ability to communicate with and govern the universal Church, describing communications as already severely restricted. He criticizes the selective application of non-intervention, arguing that it leaves aggression unpunished while permitting outside support for rebellion. He appeals to European rulers and Catholic peoples for assistance, presenting defense of papal rights as linked to the security of lawful governments generally, and concludes with prayer for protection and opponents’ conversion.

## Key themes

- Defense of international volunteers in papal service
- Condemnation of invasion and tribute to fallen soldiers
- Critique of selective non-intervention and appeal to European rulers
