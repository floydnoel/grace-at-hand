# Perpensis circumstantiis · English editorial summary

## Summary

This declaration adapts Pius VI's emergency faculties to the displacement of French bishops, administrators, and priests. Earlier indults had authorized the reconciliation of repentant clergy who had taken the revolutionary oath or occupied ecclesiastical offices without legitimate appointment, but territorial restrictions had made those powers ineffective in exile.

Bishops and diocesan administrators may now absolve their own diocesan priests even when they reside elsewhere or outside the French king's jurisdiction. Delegated confessors must be approved by the ordinary where the penitents live, and the earlier indults' other conditions remain binding. The declaration also extends these and other previously granted faculties for one year from their respective expiration dates, provided the emergency continues.

## Key themes

- Reconciliation of repentant oath-taking and unauthorized clergy
- Exercise of diocesan faculties beyond territorial boundaries
- Local approval of delegated confessors
- Conditional one-year extension of emergency indults
