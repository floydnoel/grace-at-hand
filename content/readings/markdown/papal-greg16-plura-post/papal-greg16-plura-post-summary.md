# Plura post · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI announces a universal jubilee intended to unite Catholics in prayer and penance for relief from the Church's troubles. Following the practice of earlier popes at the beginning of pontificates and during crises, he offers a plenary indulgence connected with designated church visits, fasting, confession, Communion, and almsgiving. The Roman observance has a specified three-week period; local authorities are to arrange the corresponding observance elsewhere.

The brief makes provision for travelers, enclosed religious, prisoners, the sick, and others unable to complete the prescribed works, allowing authorized substitutions suited to their circumstances. It broadens the choice of approved confessors and grants exceptional faculties for absolution and commutation of some vows, while carefully excluding specified censures, obligations, and irregularities. Bishops and other pastors must publish and explain the jubilee, designate churches, and prepare their people through preaching. The detailed historical regulations serve the common purpose of repentance, confident prayer, and restored ecclesial peace.

## Key themes

- Universal prayer and penance during ecclesiastical distress
- Sacramental and charitable works prescribed for the jubilee
- Accommodations for those unable to perform the usual works
- Exceptional confessional faculties with explicit limits
