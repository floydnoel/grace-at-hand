# Superabundavimus · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI rejoices with the archbishop of Paris over Lamennais's declaration accepting the teaching of the papal encyclical. He asks the archbishop to transmit the accompanying letter to Lamennais and thanks him for the counsel and pastoral effort that helped secure this result. The pope sees restored priestly concord and public tranquility as the principal fruits of the reconciliation.

He also commends the comparable professions made by Gerbezio and Lacordaire and hopes others will follow. A particular concern is the removal of remaining tension between Lamennais and the bishop of Rennes, to whom Gregory is also writing. The letter presents the outcome as God's work and asks for continued divine assistance so that the settlement may strengthen sound doctrine and lasting Catholic peace.

## Key themes

- The archbishop’s contribution to reconciliation
- Professions of adherence by Lamennais and associates
- Healing tensions with the bishop of Rennes
