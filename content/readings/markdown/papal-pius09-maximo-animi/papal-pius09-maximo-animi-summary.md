# Maximo animi · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX renews his protest against the overthrow of papal government in Emilia after his earlier warning of June 1859. He describes the installation of Sardinian officials, dismissal of loyal officeholders, interference with charitable institutions, and harassment of clergy. He particularly rejects an assembly in Bologna that declared an end to papal rule and union with the king of Sardinia, disputing its claim to express the population’s unanimous will.

The allocution also condemns anti-papal publications and theatrical ridicule of religion, which Pius sees as contradicting the revolutionaries’ professed respect for his spiritual authority. He praises clergy and inhabitants whom he considers faithful to the Holy See. Invoking his obligation to preserve the Church’s rights and temporal patrimony for his successors, he declares the revolutionary acts void and recalls the censures incurred by their promoters and supporters. He ends by praying for the correction of those involved, including those he believes have been deceived.

## Key themes

- Protest against the Bologna assembly and proposed Sardinian annexation
- Defense of papal civil and ecclesiastical authority
- Praise of loyal clergy and prayer for opponents
