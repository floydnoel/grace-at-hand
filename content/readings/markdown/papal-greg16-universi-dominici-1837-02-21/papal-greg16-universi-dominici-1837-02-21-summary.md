# Universi dominici · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI commissions Bishop Giuseppe Angelo Fazio to organize the new apostolic vicariate of Egypt and Arabia, separated from the vicar of Aleppo's spiritual jurisdiction. The intended work includes care of existing missions, revival of closed ones, and possible expansion toward Nubia and Abyssinia.

Relieving Fazio of his coadjutorship in Hindustan, the pope appoints him visitor and apostolic administrator directly subject to Rome. He is to inspect conditions, identify what the new vicariate needs, correct abuses in the Franciscan mission of Upper Egypt, and propose or establish suitable provisional regulations. The acts of his visitation and administrative measures must be sent to Propaganda Fide for its consideration. Those affected are instructed to cooperate with his commission.

## Key themes

- Separation of Egypt and Arabia from Aleppo’s missionary jurisdiction
- Visitation and provisional organization of the new vicariate
- Review and reform of Upper Egypt’s mission
