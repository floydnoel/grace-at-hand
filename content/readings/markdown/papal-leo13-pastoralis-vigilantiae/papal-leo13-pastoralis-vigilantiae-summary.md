# Pastoralis vigilantiae · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XIII commends the recent Catholic congress at Braga and urges the Portuguese bishops to turn its agreement into lasting pastoral cooperation. Alongside congresses that include lay participants, he recommends annual meetings of bishops. Shared experience and counsel should strengthen their work, encourage the laity, and prepare the way for diocesan and provincial synods and a national council.

He proposes an agenda covering priestly renewal, seminary formation, catechesis, Catholic publications, charitable works, and the establishment and support of religious communities. These communities, he argues, can assist local ministry and supply missionaries for territories under Portuguese rule. His praise of that overseas mission reflects the encyclical’s own view of Portugal’s imperial role. Leo also asks the government to remove restrictions on religious societies. He presents freedom for the Church as beneficial to public morality, civic responsibility, and social peace, and calls the bishops to steady implementation of the congress’s decisions.

## Key themes

- Annual episcopal meetings alongside clergy and lay congresses
- Coordinated education, catechesis, publishing, and charity
- Religious communities, overseas missions, and freedom of action
