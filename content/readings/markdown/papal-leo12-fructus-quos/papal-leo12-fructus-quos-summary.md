# Fructus Quos · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII approves a new initiative of France's Catholic Society of Good Books to encourage authors, including through prizes, to produce works of religious and secular learning that support Catholic faith. He especially hopes that readers studying the natural sciences will come to honor God as creator and deepen their acceptance of religious doctrine and moral duties.

The pope emphasizes that sound learning should be communicated with patience and charity. Invoking Augustine, he urges the society's members and writers to oppose errors without pride or cruelty toward those who hold them, and to pray for the people they correct. The brief therefore joins approval of Catholic publishing with guidance about the manner of intellectual controversy.

## Key themes

- Incentives for religious and secular works supportive of faith
- Natural science as an occasion to honor the creator
- Patient correction and charity toward opponents
