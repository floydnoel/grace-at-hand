# Ex debito pastoralis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI restores Huguang as an autonomous apostolic vicariate. Bishop Gioacchino Salvetti, vicar of Shanxi and Shaanxi, and his coadjutor have explained that their numerous and extensive territories prevent adequate administration of this additional province, which had been entrusted to their care in 1762.

Accepting their request after consultation with Propaganda Fide, the pope ends that administration and reestablishes Huguang's earlier separate status. A new bishop and apostolic vicar is to be selected and appointed by the Holy See, with the customary faculties of the office. The brief treats territorial reorganization as a means of making pastoral care practicable across a vast missionary region.

## Key themes

- Overextended missionary administration
- Restoration of Huguang’s autonomous jurisdiction
- Provision for its own bishop and apostolic vicar
