# Universi dominici · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI establishes a new episcopal see at Dubuque in response to the bishops gathered at Baltimore's provincial council in April 1837. Their petition points to the growth of the recently founded city and the pastoral benefit of a separate jurisdiction for the western territory then administered from Saint Louis.

The brief detaches the designated territory west of the Mississippi from Saint Louis and places it under a distinct bishop at Dubuque. The Holy See will appoint the bishop and grant faculties appropriate to his ministry. The act follows the advice of Propaganda Fide and revises the earlier diocesan arrangement to provide closer ecclesiastical leadership for this developing region.

## Key themes

- A new episcopal see for a growing western settlement
- Territorial separation from the Saint Louis diocese
- Papal appointment of a local bishop
