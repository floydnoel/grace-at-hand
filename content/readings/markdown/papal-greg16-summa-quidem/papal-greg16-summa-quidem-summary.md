# Summa quidem · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI welcomes the Bohemian bishops' desire that Mary be honored as conceived without original sin. He describes his own devotion and the permission already given for the word immaculate to be used of her conception in the Mass preface in his chapel and Rome's churches, as well as in the Roman clergy's calendar.

Referring to accompanying rescripts of the Congregation of Rites, he expresses satisfaction at answering their petitions for increased devotion in their dioceses. The letter encourages the bishops to cultivate Marian piety among the faithful and ask Mary's intercession for the pope's demanding ministry. It records liturgical permissions and devotional encouragement rather than presenting a new solemn doctrinal definition.

## Key themes

- Liturgical language honoring Mary’s immaculate conception
- Permissions supporting diocesan Marian devotion
- Prayer for the pope through Mary’s intercession
