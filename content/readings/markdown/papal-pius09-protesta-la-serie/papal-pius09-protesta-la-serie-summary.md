# Protesta La serie · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX formally protests the Roman Constituent Assembly’s decree of 9 February declaring papal temporal rule ended and establishing the Roman Republic. Speaking before the cardinals and diplomatic representatives at Gaeta, he declares the measure null, as he had earlier revolutionary acts. He asks the envoys to transmit his protest to their governments.

The pope portrays the change as the work of a faction oppressing his subjects and appeals for their relief. He maintains that the Holy See’s longstanding temporal sovereignty is necessary, under the existing providential order, for the free exercise of its Catholic apostolate. International expressions of support encourage his expectation that friendly nations will defend what he regards as a cause of justice.

## Key themes

- Protest against the assembly’s deposition of papal temporal rule
- Diplomatic transmission of the papal claim
- Territorial sovereignty presented as necessary to apostolic independence
