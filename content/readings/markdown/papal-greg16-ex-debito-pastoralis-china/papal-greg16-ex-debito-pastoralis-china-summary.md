# Ex debito pastoralis China · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI reorganizes the pastoral government of Zhejiang and Jiangxi after the apostolic vicar of Fujian, Rocco-Giuseppe Carpena, reports that the demands of his extensive mission prevent him from administering them adequately. The two provinces had been under his administration since 1798, and he voluntarily asks that the Holy See provide another arrangement.

Following consultation with Propaganda Fide, the operative decree establishes a distinct apostolic vicariate in each province. Each is to receive an apostolic vicar chosen by the Holy See and the faculties ordinarily granted to missionary vicars in those regions. The act responds to a practical burden of government by providing separate local leadership.

## Key themes

- Relief of an overextended apostolic vicar
- Separate vicariates for two Chinese provinces
- Papal appointment and customary missionary faculties
