# Literae fraternitatis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VIII answers Archbishop Raimondo of Guatemala concerning a persistent schism and the activities attributed to the priest Matías Delgado. The archbishop had reported that Delgado ignored Leo XII's warnings and purported to grant matrimonial dispensations reserved to the Holy See. He had requested a formal sentence against Delgado as a promoter of schism.

The pope explains that a special congregation of cardinals examined the report, supporting documents, and earlier acts before he granted the request. He sends the resulting sentence with this letter and directs the archbishop to notify those concerned and instruct the faithful prudently about its requirements. The letter itself does not reproduce that separate sentence's detailed terms. It closes by encouraging the archbishop to persevere patiently in his difficult pastoral office and promising prayer for the fruitfulness of his work.

## Key themes

- Roman examination of allegations of schism
- Reserved matrimonial dispensations and ecclesiastical authority
- Notification of a separately transmitted sentence
- Perseverance in an afflicted episcopal ministry
