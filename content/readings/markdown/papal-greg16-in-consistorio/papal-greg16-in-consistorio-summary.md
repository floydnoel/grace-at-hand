# In consistorio · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI explains the arrangements accompanying Francesco's appointment as patriarch of Lisbon. A separate apostolic letter allows him to begin exercising his pastoral office immediately, avoiding obstacles arising from the presence of two cathedral chapters: one canonically constituted but prevented from functioning, and another that the pope regards as unlawfully substituted. Gregory praises the new patriarch's refusal to recognize the latter and his willingness to submit his decisions to Rome.

The pope also rejects a local practice by which a royal nominee could govern a vacant see through chapter authority before receiving papal appointment. He argues that this would make ecclesiastical government dependent on civil choice and impede rejection of an unsuitable candidate. The letter closes with confidence that the patriarch will restore canonical order in the chapter and help repair the wider disorders of his Church.

## Key themes

- Immediate exercise of patriarchal ministry
- Competing chapters and restoration of canonical order
- Rejection of civil control through preappointment administration
