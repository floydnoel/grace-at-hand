# Optatissimus tandem · English editorial summary

## Summary

Reunited with the cardinals after his return to Rome, Pius VII gives thanks for deliverance from captivity and for the fidelity shown during the Church's trials. He recalls the devotion and generosity he witnessed in Italy and France and particularly honors those who visited or supported him at Savona. He also thanks the rulers whose alliance and military efforts helped restore peace, together with hosts who sheltered displaced bishops and Roman clergy.

The pope interprets endurance under persecution as evidence of divine assistance and invokes Mary, Peter and Paul, and the exiled popes Silverius and Martin. Gratitude must now produce greater vigilance and service. He reports measures already undertaken: suppressing secret societies in the Papal States, restoring the Society of Jesus, reopening religious houses, and gathering expelled nuns back into their monasteries. These beginnings leave extensive reconstruction still necessary. He therefore asks the cardinals for increasing practical help in repairing the Church's losses and resuming its work.

## Key themes

- Thanksgiving for liberation and reunion with the cardinals
- Lay hospitality and support for persecuted clergy
- Restoration of Jesuits and religious communities
- Continued pastoral rebuilding after political upheaval
