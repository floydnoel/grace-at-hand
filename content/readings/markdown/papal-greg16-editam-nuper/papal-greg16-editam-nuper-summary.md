# Editam nuper · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI tells the bishop of Rennes that he has joyfully received Lamennais's published declaration accepting without reservation the doctrine of the pope's encyclical. He regards this submission as a work of divine mercy and hopes that the disputes and distress surrounding the controversy will now subside.

The pope encourages the bishop to receive Lamennais favorably in response to his request. Reconciliation, he explains, should remove obstacles to their common joy and avoid renewed public controversy. Gregory also thanks the bishop for his fidelity, diligence, and activity throughout a difficult affair, and extends his blessing to him and his flock.

## Key themes

- Acceptance of papal teaching after controversy
- Episcopal welcome and the hope of restored concord
