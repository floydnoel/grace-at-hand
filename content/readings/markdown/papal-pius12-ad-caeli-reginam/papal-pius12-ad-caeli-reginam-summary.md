## Summary

*Ad Caeli Reginam* is a 1954 encyclical by Pope Pius XII that institutes the liturgical feast of the Queenship of Mary, to be celebrated on May 31. Drawing on Scripture, patristic tradition, liturgy, and theology, the pope establishes that Mary is Queen not only because she is the Mother of Christ the King, but also because of her unique cooperation in the work of redemption as the New Eve. He presents the queenship as a source of confidence, peace, and filial devotion for the universal Church.

## Historical Context

The document was issued at the close of the Marian Year (1954), the centenary of the definition of the Immaculate Conception (1854), and four years after Pius XII had defined the Assumption of Mary (1950). The world was still recovering from World War II, with cities in ruins and moral decay spreading. The Church faced rising secularism, persecution of Christians in communist countries, and the threat of global instability. Pius XII saw in Mary's maternal queenship a rallying point for peace and a remedy for the era's spiritual and social crises.

## Key Themes

- **Divine Motherhood as the foundation of queenship** — Mary is Queen because she bore the King of Kings, Jesus Christ.
- **Co-redemption and the New Eve** — Mary's intimate association with Christ in the work of salvation gives her a share in his royal dignity; she freely offered her Son on Golgotha, cooperating in the redemption of the human race.
- **Intercessory power and spiritual motherhood** — Mary, seated at her Son's right hand, intercedes with a mother's love and "cannot be refused"; she distributes the graces of the Redeemer.
- **Universal sovereignty** — Mary is Queen of heaven and earth, of every creature, and above all angels and saints; her rule is merciful and motherly, not political.
- **Devotional practice** — The encyclical commends the Rosary, the Litany of Loreto, consecration to the Immaculate Heart, and frequenting Marian shrines and feasts.
- **Caution against excess** — While exalting Mary, the pope warns against exaggerated or unfounded opinions as well as narrow minimizing of her dignity; the living Magisterium is the norm of truth.

## Significance

*Ad Caeli Reginam* formally established the Feast of the Queenship of Mary in the universal calendar, giving liturgical expression to a devotion that had existed since the early Church. It synthesized patristic, medieval, and modern teaching on Mary's role in salvation, linking her queenship to her divine motherhood and her cooperation in redemption. The encyclical stands as a key document in the high Mariology of the 20th century, alongside *Munificentissimus Deus* (Assumption) and *Mystici Corporis*, and remains a touchstone for Catholic teaching on the Blessed Virgin's place in the economy of salvation.
