# Cum Multa · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII entrusts the Roman College once more to the Society of Jesus, recalling its educational achievements and Pius VII's intention after restoring the society. The grant includes Saint Ignatius's church, the Caravita oratory, museums, library, observatory, and associated property. The Jesuits must reopen the public schools and add chairs of sacred eloquence and physical and chemical science.

The brief provides annual public funding, preserves degree-granting privileges and the Marian congregation, and directs publication of astronomical observations and other useful scholarly work. It combines intellectual education with religious instruction and pastoral service in the college's congregations and oratory. Cardinal Pacca is charged with the transfer so that classes can reopen on schedule. Leo also assigns the Jesuits direction of a planned college for noble pupils and its associated Tivoli residences, while praying that the institutions benefit both the Church and civil society.

## Key themes

- Restoration of Jesuit leadership of the Roman College
- Public teaching, scientific study, and pastoral formation
- Funding, privileges, and arrangements for reopening
- A further educational commission for noble pupils
