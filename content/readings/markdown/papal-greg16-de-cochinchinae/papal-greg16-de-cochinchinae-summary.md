# De Cochinchinae · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI establishes emergency arrangements for the leadership of the Cochinchina mission amid continuing persecution. He refers to a separate appointment of Dominique Lefebvre to succeed Bishop Étienne-Théodore Cuenot in specified circumstances, then considers the danger that the bishop and those designated to succeed him might die.

The brief authorizes the apostolic vicar of Western Tonkin to administer Cochinchina temporarily if the specified contingency occurs and to choose a suitable missionary from Tonkin or Cochinchina as a new vicar. He is also empowered to confer the episcopal dignity and the deceased prelate's title, with necessary faculties and the reservations belonging to the Holy See. The purpose is continuity of missionary government despite disrupted communications and threatened leadership.

## Key themes

- Contingency planning during persecution
- Temporary administration by the vicar of Western Tonkin
- Delegated selection of a successor and episcopal provision
