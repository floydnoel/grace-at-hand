# Qui Pacem · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII reflects on the risen Christ's greeting of peace to disciples who had abandoned him and struggled to believe in his resurrection. He presents that merciful encounter as an offer also extended to his hearers. True peace, he argues, requires reconciliation with God, oneself, and one's neighbor; indulgence of disordered desires instead intensifies inner conflict and estrangement.

The homily connects moral conversion with faith in the resurrection. Leo defends the apostolic testimony by emphasizing the witnesses' initial reluctance, their direct encounter with Christ, their agreement, and their willingness to endure suffering for what they proclaimed. He urges prayer for unbelievers and calls believers to make their conduct conform to their faith. Repentance for greed, lust, pride, and failures of mutual love removes obstacles to the peace Christ promises. The address ends with hope that peace received now will be preserved to eternal life.

## Key themes

- The risen Christ’s merciful greeting to failing disciples
- Resurrection testimony and the call to faith
- Repentance, disciplined desires, and reconciliation with others
