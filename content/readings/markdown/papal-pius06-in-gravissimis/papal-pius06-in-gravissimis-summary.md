# In gravissimis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VI grants French bishops and diocesan administrators in communion with Rome a broad, carefully conditioned set of emergency faculties. Their requests reflect the difficulty of maintaining pastoral care during revolutionary disruption. Acts performed under the grant must expressly identify the authority delegated by the Apostolic See and observe its governing discipline.

The attached schedule covers absolution from reserved sins and censures, including reconciliation of oath-taking clergy after public retraction and reparation; dispensations from specified irregularities and matrimonial impediments; adjustments to ordination ages and schedules; and provision of benefices when ordinary staffing rules cannot be met. It also addresses displaced religious, transfers between institutes, supervision of women's houses when regular superiors cannot act, and adaptation or transfer of indulgences. Subdelegation to suitable priests is permitted for faculties that do not require episcopal consecration. The grant lasts one year if the emergency continues and is originally restricted to the recipients' own dioceses and places under the French king's authority. The document combines extraordinary flexibility with explicit accountability and limits.

## Key themes

- Delegated authority for pastoral care during the French crisis
- Reconciliation conditioned on public retraction and reparation
- Dispensations, religious communities, and sacramental provision
- Territorial, temporal, and subdelegation limits
