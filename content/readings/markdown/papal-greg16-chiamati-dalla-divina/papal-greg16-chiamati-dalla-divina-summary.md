# Chiamati dalla divina · English editorial summary

## Summary

At the beginning of his pontificate, Gregory XVI addresses subjects of the Papal States affected by internal unrest. He presents his temporal rule in paternal terms and says that, despite limited resources and wider ecclesiastical responsibilities, he has begun considering measures to alleviate hardship, especially among the poor. Reports of disturbances interrupt these plans and cause him particular distress.

The pope appeals directly to those who have withdrawn from his obedience, assuring them of his continuing affection and readiness for mercy and pardon. He urges them to recognize the insecurity brought by disorder and return to religious fidelity, submission, and concord. Although he acknowledges that rigorous measures could become necessary, his stated preference is reconciliation accompanied by provisions for public welfare. The letter closes with prayer that peace will allow his subjects to benefit from both spiritual consolation and material improvement.

## Key themes

- Paternal government and relief of poverty
- Mercy and reconciliation for subjects involved in unrest
- Religious obedience, concord, and public peace
