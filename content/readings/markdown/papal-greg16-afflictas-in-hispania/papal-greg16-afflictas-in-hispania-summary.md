# Afflictas in Hispania · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI renews his protest against the Spanish government's treatment of the Church, explaining that earlier restraint has not secured improvement. He lists restrictions on ordinations, suppression and confiscation of monasteries, proposed sales of churches, and measures making clergy dependent on government stipends. He also challenges the exclusion of ecclesiastical exiles from permission to return and civil intervention in diocesan administration and doctrinal proceedings.

A central case is the removal and expulsion of Giuseppe Ramirez de Arellano after his protests as vicegerent of the papal nunciature. Gregory connects that action, and the cessation of the ecclesiastical Rota's work, with an attempt to silence defenses of Church rights. Asserting the Church's authority over doctrine, ministry, worship, property, and appeals to Rome, he condemns the contested measures and declares them without ecclesiastical effect. He praises bishops, clergy, and laity who have maintained canonical allegiance and asks the cardinals to join in prayer for Spain's afflicted Church.

## Key themes

- Government restrictions on ordinations, religious houses, and Church property
- Civil interference in doctrinal judgments and episcopal governance
- Expulsion of the nunciature's vicegerent and suppression of its tribunal
- Fidelity to canonically constituted pastors
