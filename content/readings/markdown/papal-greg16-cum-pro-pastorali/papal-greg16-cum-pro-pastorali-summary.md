# Cum pro pastorali · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI returns to the condition of the Portuguese Church after earlier protests failed to produce improvement. He describes confiscated or auctioned Church property, closed churches, expelled or confined ministers, and the removal of papal representatives. He particularly objects to a civil tribunal reforming ecclesiastical affairs, government control over appointments and sacramental ministry, the suppression of religious houses, and the removal of Lisbon's patriarchal title.

The address argues that these measures invade authority belonging to the Church and threaten its constitution and freedom. Gregory condemns the decrees and declares them without ecclesiastical effect, recalling the canonical penalties attached to violating sacred rights and property. He warns that continued injury may require more severe spiritual action, while expressing a strong desire to avoid it through repentance and reparation. Invoking Peter's deliverance from imprisonment, he asks for the Church's release from the constraints imposed upon it.

## Key themes

- Confiscations, suppression of religious houses, and expelled clergy
- Civil control over ecclesiastical governance and ministry
- Canonical protest, warning of spiritual sanctions, and appeal for repentance
