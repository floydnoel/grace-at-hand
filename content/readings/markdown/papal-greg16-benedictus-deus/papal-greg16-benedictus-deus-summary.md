# Benedictus Deus · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI defines the territories of the Catholic dioceses in the United States in response to episcopal requests and the wishes expressed at the 1833 provincial council of Baltimore. He welcomes the growth of Catholic life but explains that the creation of new sees has left earlier boundaries uncertain or unsuitable. Clear territorial limits are intended to remove doubts about jurisdiction and support cooperation among bishops and priests.

The bull specifies the extent of Baltimore and twelve other dioceses, commonly by states or territories and, in New Jersey and Illinois, by more detailed divisions. It also places Richmond under the administration of Baltimore's archbishop pending another arrangement. The archbishop of Baltimore is appointed to publish and execute the provisions, resolve objections through the authority granted him, and transmit authenticated records of implementation to Rome. The document thus combines a national territorial settlement with explicit procedures for putting it into effect.

## Key themes

- Clear diocesan territories amid Catholic growth
- State, county, and territorial boundaries
- Baltimore’s responsibility for executing and documenting the settlement
