# Sextus iam · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI addresses the cardinals as he enters the sixth year of his pontificate, lamenting continued attacks on ecclesiastical freedom. In Portugal, he denounces efforts to promote schism, including an attempted prohibition of communion with the Holy See under threat of excommunication. He presents these developments as further reason for defending the Church's authority.

The address then explains his unsuccessful effort to maintain a purely religious papal representation in Spain amid conflict over political power. Government conditions left his delegate unable to defend Church rights, and Gregory eventually recalled him. He lists civil interference in book censorship, suppression and confiscation of religious houses, displacement of pastors, and restrictions on ordinations. To prevent the delegate's silence being interpreted as papal consent, he publicly rejects the measures and declares them without ecclesiastical effect. The address concludes by asking Mary's intercession for peace in the afflicted Church.

## Key themes

- Threatened separation from Rome in Portugal
- Limits and recall of the papal representative in Spain
- Public rejection of government encroachments on Church life
