# Quae nuncia · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI consoles the Christians of Tonkin and Cochinchina after reports of the deaths of bishops and other ministers amid intensifying persecution. He names Delgado, Henares, and Havard and expresses both admiration for their witness and anxiety for communities deprived of their pastors. He assures the faithful that he is working to provide replacement leaders and continues to pray for their strength.

The letter urges Catholics to preserve their faith despite threats, torture, and fear of death, looking to Christ's suffering and the example of their bishops. Gregory also addresses those who have denied the faith under pressure, calling them to repentance and confidence in divine mercy. To those who remain steadfast, he offers the hope of eternal life and eventual relief from persecution, joining paternal affection with an appeal for endurance.

## Key themes

- Consolation after the loss of missionary bishops and ministers
- Continuity of pastoral leadership during persecution
- Perseverance for the steadfast and repentance for those who have fallen
