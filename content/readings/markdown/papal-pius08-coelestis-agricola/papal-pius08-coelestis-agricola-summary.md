# Coelestis agricola · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VIII condemns the civil establishment of a bishopric at San Salvador and the appointment of the parish priest Matías Delgado to govern it without papal authorization. He recounts repeated warnings from the archbishop of Guatemala and Leo XII, including a final period allowed for repentance. The pope presents Delgado's continued exercise of spiritual jurisdiction as a threat to ecclesiastical unity and to the rights of the legitimate bishop and the Holy See.

The brief declares the purported diocese, Delgado's nomination, and his jurisdictional acts invalid. It declares Delgado and those responsible for or supporting the undertaking subject to major excommunication and other canonical penalties, reserving absolution to the pope or his successors apart from the stated deathbed exception and requiring public retraction and satisfaction. Nevertheless, it suspends the sentence's effect for thirty days after notification, giving another opportunity for reconciliation. The faithful are directed to reject Delgado's claimed pastoral authority and avoid receiving sacraments from him or ministers acting on his authorization. Pius describes the disciplinary action as intended to secure repentance and protect the flock.

## Key themes

- Invalidity of a bishopric erected by civil authority
- Delgado's claimed jurisdiction and the unity of the Church
- Excommunication, reserved absolution, and public reparation
- A final thirty-day suspension for repentance
