# Inter ea · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI addresses the Swiss bishops about governmental decrees suppressing monasteries and transferring, selling, or otherwise disposing of their property. He deplores Catholic participation in these measures and recalls the monasteries' contributions to worship, pastoral care, education, and relief of the poor. He also points to protections established by public agreement and praises local administrations that have resisted suppression and sales.

Renewing his protest, the pope declares alienations made without the Holy See's consent invalid before the Church, whether already completed or still proposed. He directs the bishops to withhold cooperation and prudently warn recipients that, under the ecclesiastical judgment he states, they cannot retain or acquire such goods with a clear conscience. At the same time he asks them to work with patience and pastoral charity for the reconsideration of those Catholics involved in the measures.

## Key themes

- Religious and social contributions of Swiss monasteries
- Historical canonical judgment on unauthorized property transfers
- Prudent episcopal resistance and patient pastoral correction
