# Cum populi · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VI sends the bishop of Aleria copies of his fuller letter to the French hierarchy and faithful concerning the Civil Constitution of the Clergy. He wants the bishop, cathedral chapter, parish priests, clergy, and people to understand their respective obligations during the disruption of ecclesiastical government. In the pope's judgment, accepting the constitution endangers Catholic doctrine and discipline and exposes adherents to schism and canonical penalties.

The brief calls for vigilant pastoral action as persecution increases. Its closing notice records that copies were also sent to the bishops of Ajaccio, Sagone, Nebbio, and Mariana, extending this warning among the Corsican dioceses.

## Key themes

- Transmission of papal guidance to the Corsican bishops
- Duties of clergy and faithful under the Civil Constitution
- Pastoral vigilance against schism
