# Dum intima · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI protests the forcible removal and confinement of Archbishop Clemens August of Cologne following his refusal to accept a government-backed interpretation of Catholic discipline on mixed marriages. He explains that the archbishop had adhered to Pius VIII's apostolic letter of 1830, which Gregory regards as having already extended toleration to its permissible limit.

The address recounts papal attempts to correct the disputed interpretation and cites a dying bishop's retraction of instructions adopted under civil pressure. Gregory says the Cologne archbishop was then required either to follow the rejected interpretation or leave office, and was removed after refusing. He condemns the violation of ecclesiastical immunity and jurisdiction, praises the archbishop's steadfastness, and publicly repudiates practices contrary to the authentic meaning of Pius VIII's letter. The cardinals are invited to pray for relief from the wider troubles afflicting the Church.

## Key themes

- Prussian pressure on Catholic mixed-marriage discipline
- Removal and confinement of Cologne’s archbishop
- Papal interpretation of earlier concessions and defense of ecclesiastical jurisdiction
