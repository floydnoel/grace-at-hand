# La moltiplicità · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI directs stricter handling of requests for marriage dispensations within specified close degrees of consanguinity or affinity. He fears that easily obtained concessions can encourage misconduct and turn a dispensation into a means of escaping consequences. Only genuinely canonical grounds and circumstances of exceptional necessity should justify relaxation of the prohibition; alleged threats or difficulties that can be addressed otherwise are insufficient.

The letter requires ordinaries, where possible personally, to state the particular grounds and circumstances instead of relying on formulaic testimonials, and to verify them when executing a grant. It prescribes suitable penances where scandal needs repair and insists that agents present facts truthfully. Gregory renews penalties against falsification and attempts to obtain a concession from another tribunal after refusal. Cardinal Pacca is to communicate these requirements to Italian ordinaries and the Apostolic Datary and withhold improperly prepared petitions.

## Key themes

- Canonical necessity for close-kinship dispensations
- Personal responsibility of ordinaries for evidence and verification
- Penances for scandal and sanctions against fraudulent petitions
