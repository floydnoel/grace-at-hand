# Pastorale officium · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI appoints Luigi Fontana, apostolic vicar of Sichuan, to lead the newly restored autonomous vicariate of Huguang. Referring to a separate act ending its administration by the vicar of Shanxi, the pope selects Fontana for his piety, prudence, learning, religious zeal, and long missionary experience in China.

The brief transfers him from Sichuan and grants the rights and faculties proper to his new office, while preserving Propaganda Fide's authority. It also provides for continuity in the vacated mission by allowing Fontana's coadjutor, the titular bishop of Maxula, to succeed him in Sichuan. The faithful and others concerned are directed to accept his government and carry out his lawful pastoral instructions.

## Key themes

- Experienced missionary leadership for restored Huguang
- Transfer of an apostolic vicar between Chinese missions
- Succession arrangements for the Sichuan vicariate
