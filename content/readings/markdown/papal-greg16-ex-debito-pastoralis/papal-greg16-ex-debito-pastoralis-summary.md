# Ex debito pastoralis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI establishes an apostolic vicariate for Japan and the Liu-Kiu islands, together with adjacent islands not already subject to another prelate. He grounds the decision in renewed hope that missionaries can enter Japan and revive Catholic life after devastating persecution.

The brief identifies access through the Liu-Kiu islands as more promising than an approach from Korea. To consolidate the mission from its beginning and meet its dangers and needs, Gregory calls for leadership possessing episcopal dignity. Acting after consultation with Propaganda Fide, he gives the new jurisdiction continuing legal force under the direction of the pope and the Holy See. No individual vicar is named in this act.

## Key themes

- A renewed missionary opening after persecution
- Access through the Liu-Kiu islands
- Episcopal government for a mission in its initial stages
