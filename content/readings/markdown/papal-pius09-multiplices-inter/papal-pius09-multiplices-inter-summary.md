# Multiplices inter · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX creates the diocese of Portoviejo from the Ecuadorian provinces of Esmeraldas and Manabí, detached from Quito and Guayaquil. Responding to the Ecuadorian president’s request and the difficulty of governing extensive territories, he makes Portoviejo the episcopal city and elevates its parish church to a cathedral, retaining its parish functions. The new bishop is to be a suffragan of Quito.

The bull lays out the diocese’s practical and canonical organization under the existing concordat. It provides for episcopal authority, possible new parishes, a cathedral chapter, a seminary, residences, and financial support. Specified annual revenues are assigned to the bishop, seminary, and cathedral, with additional arrangements for clerical appointments, property, and administration during a vacancy. Pius reserves the possibility of future territorial subdivision and charges Serafino Vannutelli with implementation. An authenticated report and map must be sent to the Holy See, recording the new diocese’s establishment and boundaries.

## Key themes

- Separation of territory and establishment of an episcopal see
- Cathedral, chapter, seminary, and financial provision
- Implementation under the Ecuadorian concordat and reporting to Rome
