# Ubi novam · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI responds to reports and writings concerning a new religious association in the diocese of Bayeux. He describes its members as claiming apostolic authority, extraordinary conversations with heavenly beings, visions, and miracles. He especially rejects their proposal of a third kingdom of the Holy Spirit in which allegedly unexplained Gospel truths would be revealed and the Church restored from supposed corruption.

The pope connects these claims with a figure styling himself chief of Normandy, without naming him in the letter. He approves the bishop's prompt opposition and his use of pastoral letters and warnings to mobilize the clergy. Gregory urges continued vigilance so that Catholics remain firm in their faith and reject the association's teachings and pretensions. The response rests on the documents submitted and on earlier writings already known to Rome.

## Key themes

- Claims to a new lay apostolate and extraordinary revelation
- Rejection of a proposed third kingdom of the Holy Spirit
- Papal support for episcopal warnings and clergy vigilance
