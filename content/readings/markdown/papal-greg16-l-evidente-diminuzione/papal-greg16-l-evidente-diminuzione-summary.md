# L'evidente diminuzione · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI approves reforms to the Acqua Felice supply in response to scarcity and unequal distribution among users entitled to specified quantities. A commission has investigated diminished springs, obstructions in the collecting works, and the mismatch between existing concessions and available water. The pope supports adding suitable springs and repairing the system while ensuring that any unavoidable shortage is shared proportionately.

The chirograph endorses the technical plan developed by inspector Cavalieri and reviewed by Roman academic and engineering bodies. Cardinal Ludovico Gazzoli receives authority to implement uniform measuring and regulating arrangements, including separate distribution tanks, regular outlets, and control mechanisms at major reservoirs. The purpose is to replace irregular practice with a stable system that distributes water fairly during scarcity and remains usable when abundance returns. The act combines public utility, technical consultation, and enforceable administrative powers.

## Key themes

- Fair allocation of water during scarcity
- Repairing supply works and adding springs
- Expert review and standardized hydraulic regulation
