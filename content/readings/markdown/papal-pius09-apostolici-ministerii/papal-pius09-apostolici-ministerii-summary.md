# Apostolici ministerii · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX appoints Antonio Maria, bishop of Fabriano and Matelica, as apostolic co-visitor of the Armenian Antonian monks’ Roman community. The brief follows an earlier visitation order and the removal of the abbot general. Because the original visitor had to return to Constantinople and his Roman subdelegate subsequently resigned, Pius provides a new arrangement to ensure that the visitation proceeds.

The bishop is empowered to investigate both the spiritual and material affairs of the house and its church. Once the abbot general’s departure takes effect, existing offices in the Roman community are to cease, and valid government of its spiritual and temporal affairs is placed under the co-visitor. He may govern directly or appoint a religious superior with delegated powers. The monks, regardless of rank, are ordered to cooperate, and the visitor may proceed canonically against disobedience. The document concerns this specific intervention in an Eastern Catholic religious institute.

## Key themes

- Continuation of an ordered apostolic visitation
- Temporary concentration of local authority in the co-visitor
- Canonical obedience and reform of religious government
