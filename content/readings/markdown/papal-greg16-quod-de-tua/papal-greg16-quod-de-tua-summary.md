# Quod de tua · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI writes directly to Lamennais to welcome the declaration conveyed through Cardinal Bartolomeo, bishop of Ostia. He receives it as the fulfillment of his hope for the writer's submission and as a source of consolation after prolonged distress. The letter presents the act as a victory of humility, faith, and obedience over worldly opinion.

The pope encourages Lamennais to persevere and use his intellectual gifts to support the doctrine of the encyclical and bring others into agreement with it. He also praises the approval given to the declaration by Lamennais's disciple named in the text as Gerbezio. While anticipating further temptations to division or novelty, Gregory expresses paternal affection and prays that God will confirm this reconciliation.

## Key themes

- Acceptance of a declaration of submission
- Intellectual work in support of the encyclical
- Perseverance in reconciliation and Catholic peace
