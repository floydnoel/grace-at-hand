# Apostolici ministerii · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI raises the Dutch mission of Curaçao to a distinct apostolic vicariate. He explains that the mission's growth makes a more developed form of ecclesiastical government advantageous for the spiritual care of its Catholic population. The decision follows consultation with the cardinals responsible for Propaganda Fide.

The brief provides that the mission be administered by an apostolic vicar possessing episcopal character, chosen by the Holy See and furnished with the faculties customary for that office. Its concluding legal clauses establish the act's continuing force and set aside contrary rulings or provisions. The document supplies a new missionary jurisdiction rather than naming its first officeholder.

## Key themes

- Curaçao's transition from mission to apostolic vicariate
- Episcopal leadership and customary missionary faculties
- Papal provision for a growing Catholic community
