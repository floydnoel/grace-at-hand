# Dum Nos · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VI addresses Avignon and the Comtat Venaissin as both their pastor and claimed sovereign after the French National Assembly's annexation and administrative redivision. He praises clergy and laity who have resisted the revolutionary ecclesiastical system, interpreting even hostile French reports as evidence of their fidelity. He again rejects the authority assumed by Benedetto Francesco Malierio and ministers acting without delegation from the lawful pastors.

The letter extends to these territories the renewed admonitions already issued to France, with successive periods for repentance, and the emergency faculties granted to French bishops. These provisions distinguish pastoral assistance and reconciliation from the pope's continuing assertion of temporal rights. He condemns the annexation measures, denies that he has renounced his title or agreed to compensation, and declares the new French decrees and acts against his sovereignty invalid. Pius calls for perseverance in religion and obedience to the authority he considers legitimate, while praying for the conversion of opponents. The document combines ecclesiastical regulation with an explicit protest over territorial rule.

## Key themes

- Papal protest against French annexation and administrative changes
- Rejection of unauthorized ecclesiastical government
- Extension of admonitions and emergency pastoral faculties
- Religious fidelity and the pope's asserted sovereign rights
