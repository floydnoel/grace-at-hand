# Enciclica Pro Eximia Tua

**La Santa Sede**

---

> *Note: This document was translated from the original Italian into English. Translation provided by DeepL (July 2026).*

SHORT ENCYCLICAL OF THE SUPREME PONTIFF BENEDICT XIV PRO EXIMIA TUA To the Venerable Brother, the Archbishop of Turin. Pope Benedict XIV. To the Venerable Brother, greetings and Apostolic Blessings. Given your outstanding religious spirit and the respect you owe to canonical discipline, we have no doubt whatsoever that you, Venerable Brother—rightly esteemed for the pastoral care you have so commendably devoted for so long to the flock entrusted to you—will feel no small amount of bitterness over what We, by means of another letter (sent in the form of a Brief to all the Bishops of the Catholic world, written this very day, printed, and which will be delivered to you with this one) communicate to you, not without great sadness. Although we were aware that the detestable practice, which we so strongly condemn, in the celebration of Masses was also prevalent elsewhere, we did not, however, know that it was of such a nature and carried out with such great greed for sordid gain, as we were recently informed is being practiced by certain clergymen from the Piedmont and by laypeople. Therefore, we seek to implement a salutary remedy against such a sordid trade, so as to eradicate this stain of infamy from the dioceses of Piedmont at its very roots. In truth, so as not to give the impression of undermining the opinion that everyone holds regarding the decorum of ecclesiastical discipline and the observance that prevails in the dioceses of Piedmont, and in order to duly fulfill our duties of apostolic solicitude, we have decided to write on this matter. 1. Therefore, as you can easily deduce from this, Venerable Brother, this is a particular expression of charity toward each Bishop of Piedmont; it is therefore necessary that you exert yourself greatly so that—both because of the gravity of the situation and by reason of Our intervention—you may make every effort to ensure that We may congratulate you on the complete eradication of the mercury contagion. 2. If, however, the obstinacy of the wicked proves so stubborn that they are not at all concerned about the penalties imposed by Our very own letters, and it becomes necessary to resort to the aid of the secular authorities to eradicate it, do not fail to call upon it, confident that it will stand by your side more willingly than you might think. 3. For indeed, the deference shown toward Us and toward this Apostolic See by Our most beloved Son in Christ, Charles Emmanuel, the illustrious King of Sardinia, that every day We expect greater and more certain demonstrations of royal obedience and zeal (not only toward Ourselves, but also toward Your Brotherhood) with regard to ecclesiastical discipline, and of patronage toward Us. In the meantime, We most graciously impart the Apostolic Blessing to Your Fraternity. Given at Rome, at Saint Mary Major, under the Fisherman’s Ring, on June 30, 1741, in the first year of Our Pontificate.
