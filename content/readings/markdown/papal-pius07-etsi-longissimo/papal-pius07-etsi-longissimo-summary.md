# Etsi longissimo · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VII urges the bishops and clergy of Spanish Catholic America to oppose the uprisings occurring in their regions and to encourage obedience to King Ferdinand. He connects this appeal with the religious duty of submission to civil authorities and with his own responsibility to promote peace.

The pope directs pastors to explain the harms he associates with rebellion, commend the king's concern for religion and his subjects, and recall the sacrifices made by Spaniards in defense of religion and royal loyalty. He treats suppression of sedition and renewed fidelity to the crown as the pastoral response required in this particular conflict. His closing exhortation promises the reward of peacemakers to clergy who carry out that work. The brief expresses the pope's position toward the contemporary colonial revolts rather than offering an account of their competing political claims.

## Key themes

- Papal opposition to the American revolts against Spain
- Clerical promotion of obedience to King Ferdinand
- Religious arguments for peace and royal loyalty
