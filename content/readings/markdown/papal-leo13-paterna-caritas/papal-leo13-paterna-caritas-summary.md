# Paterna Caritas · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XIII welcomes the restoration of concord among Armenian Catholics and asks them to preserve it through obedience to their patriarch and bishops, communion with the Apostolic See, and patient, unselfish charity. He also commends loyalty to Ottoman civil authority. Their peaceful common life, he hopes, will encourage other Armenian Christians to enter communion with Rome.

To support that invitation, Leo recounts traditions and ecclesiastical precedents that he understands as evidence of Armenian attachment to the Roman See. He also reviews papal assistance to Armenian communities, including hospitality, educational institutions, episcopal arrangements, and diplomatic representation. Union, in his account, includes care for the Armenian liturgy, language, and literary heritage; he points particularly to the Armenian college in Rome and earlier efforts to preserve the rite. The encyclical ends with a call to prayer for unity through the intercession of Mary and Gregory the Enlightener, extending its hope to other Christians separated from Rome.

## Key themes

- Charity and ecclesiastical obedience after internal reconciliation
- Papal invitation to Armenian Christians outside Roman communion
- Preservation of Armenian liturgy, language, and clerical education
