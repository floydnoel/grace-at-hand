# Inter maximas · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI condemns the booklet published under Francesco Forti's name as a Letter on the Direction of Studies. He warns that its small format and low price make it particularly accessible to young readers. His criticism concerns the work's treatment of skepticism, the soul, morality, revelation, and religion's relation to political authority.

The pope argues that Forti reduces religion to a political institution governed and reformed by the state, replaces revealed moral obligations with social utility and sentiment, and weakens Catholic doctrine and ecclesiastical jurisdiction. After consulting the cardinals responsible for doctrinal examination, he prohibits the booklet in all languages and editions under the historical rules of the Index of Forbidden Books. Bishops and other ordinaries are charged with teaching Catholic doctrine and keeping the faithful from the work. The brief also orders public posting of the condemnation in Rome.

## Key themes

- A theological critique of skepticism and political religion
- Concern for young readers and accessible publications
- Historical prohibition and episcopal responsibility for instruction
