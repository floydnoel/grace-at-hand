# Quae causa · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VI calls the dioceses of the Papal States to intensified prayer as threats against the Church, Apostolic See, and papal government increase. He recalls supplications already held in Rome, urges penitence rather than discouragement, and invokes Mary, Peter, and Pius V. While reporting military preparations to defend the territory, he explicitly describes their purpose as protection rather than aggression and presents prayer as the principal defense.

The brief grants a locally arranged two-week jubilee with church visits, confession, Communion, almsgiving, fasting, and prescribed intercessions. It provides accommodations for religious communities, prisoners, the sick, and shepherds unable to leave their flocks, and permits application of the indulgence to the dead. Approved confessors receive special faculties with exclusions concerning heresy, certain vows, irregularities, and unresolved public censures. Pius encourages communal processions and prayer beyond the jubilee itself. He also extends the suspension of theaters and carnival entertainments through the following year, seeking a sustained public atmosphere of penitence during the emergency.

## Key themes

- Penitential prayer during threats to papal territory
- Defensive military preparations alongside spiritual intercession
- Jubilee observances adapted to practical impediments
- Continuing public supplications and restrictions on entertainments
