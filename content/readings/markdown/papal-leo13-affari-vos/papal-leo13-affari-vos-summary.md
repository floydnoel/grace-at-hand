## Summary

*Affari Vos* (1897) is Pope Leo XIII's apostolic letter addressing the Manitoba School Question — a conflict over whether Catholic parents in Manitoba, Canada had the right to send their children to publicly funded Catholic schools. The Pope defends the Church's position that Catholic children must receive an education in line with Catholic doctrine, denounces Manitoba's 1890 law abolishing separate Catholic schools, and calls for unity among Catholics in seeking redress.

## Historical Context

Manitoba's entry into Canadian Confederation in 1870 had guaranteed Catholic separate schools. But in 1890, the provincial legislature passed a law abolishing the separate school system and establishing a single, non-sectarian public school system, effectively stripping Catholics of state-funded religious education. This triggered a national controversy that reached the Vatican. Pope Leo XIII, a pope deeply engaged with questions of Church and state in the modern world (cf. *Rerum Novarum* on labor, *Immortale Dei* on political authority), dispatched an apostolic delegate to investigate and issued *Affari Vos* to lay down the Church's position on the inviolability of Catholic education.

## Key Themes

- **Right of Catholic education**: Children must be educated in schools that respect Catholic faith; neutral or secular schools are inherently dangerous to faith.
- **Parental rights**: Fathers have the natural and inherent right to determine the moral and religious formation of their children.
- **Condemnation of Manitoba's 1890 law**: The law is described as "noxious" and "unjust" for abolishing Catholic school rights.
- **Insufficiency of compromise**: Remedial legislation passed afterward is "defective, imperfect, insufficient."
- **Call for Catholic unity**: The greatest obstacle to justice has been internal division among Catholics, whom the Pope urges to set aside party politics.
- **Excellence in Catholic schools**: Catholic schools must match or exceed public standards in intellectual culture while remaining faithful to doctrine.
- **Role of the Catholic press**: Journalists should defend truth courageously and respectfully, deferring to episcopal authority.

## Significance

*Affari Vos* is a landmark statement on Catholic educational rights in the context of liberal secular states. It reaffirms principles later restated in Vatican II's *Gravissimum Educationis* and remains a touchstone for Church teaching on parental rights, religious liberty in education, and the Church's insistence that education cannot be religiously neutral. In Canadian history, it shaped Catholic political mobilization and contributed to ongoing constitutional debates about confessional school rights, which remain embedded in Canada's constitution under Section 93.
