# Perlatae dudum · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI acknowledges the letters and decrees submitted by the archbishop of Baltimore and his suffragan bishops after their provincial council. He explains that Propaganda Fide has examined the material and will send its response, while separate apostolic letters address particular decisions or requests. This letter itself chiefly conveys his appreciation of the bishops' religious zeal and communion with Rome.

The pope encourages them to persevere in pastoral work with hope and patience, attributing its success to God rather than their own strength. He presents their task as cultivating the portion of the Church entrusted to them and leading people to Christ. He assures them of continuing papal support and asks that his blessing be communicated to their faithful.

## Key themes

- Roman examination of provincial council decrees
- Encouragement of united and persevering pastoral work
- Divine assistance and continuing papal support
