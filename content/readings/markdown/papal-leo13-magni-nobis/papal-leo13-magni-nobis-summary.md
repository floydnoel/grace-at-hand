## Summary

*Magni Nobis* is an apostolic letter by Pope Leo XIII, issued on March 7, 1889, formally establishing the Catholic University of America in Washington, D.C. The document approves the university's statutes and constitutions, grants it the authority to confer academic degrees (including doctorates in philosophy, theology, and canon law), and outlines its governance and educational mission.

## Historical Context

The letter was written at a time of rapid growth for the Catholic Church in the United States, which was still a missionary territory in many respects. The Third Plenary Council of Baltimore (1884) had called for the creation of a national Catholic university, and construction was already underway when Leo XIII issued this decree. The centennial of the establishment of the American Catholic hierarchy (1789–1889) provided the occasion for the university's dedication. The document reflects the Church's desire to engage modern learning while safeguarding Catholic orthodoxy in an increasingly pluralistic American society.

## Key Themes

- **Papal approval of a national Catholic university**: Leo XIII formally sanctions the Catholic University of America and endows it with the rights of a lawfully constituted university.
- **Authority to confer degrees**: The university is empowered to grant degrees in philosophy, theology, canon law, and other faculties as they are established.
- **Episcopal oversight**: The Archbishop of Baltimore is appointed chancellor, and local bishops are charged with superintending the university's discipline and curriculum.
- **Doctrinal fidelity**: The curriculum, especially in philosophy and theology, must be submitted to the Holy See for examination and approval.
- **Clerical and lay education**: The university is to serve both clerical and lay students equally.
- **Mandatory canon law school**: A faculty of pontifical canon law is to be established, emphasizing its importance for the times.
- **Affiliation without absorption**: Seminaries and other Catholic institutions are encouraged to affiliate with the university while retaining their autonomy.
- **Exclusivity of foundation**: No other institution of this kind may be established without consulting the Apostolic See.

## Significance

*Magni Nobis* is the founding charter of the Catholic University of America, the national university of the Catholic Church in the United States and the only pontifical university in the country. It established a model for integrating rigorous academic study with Catholic doctrine, and it gave the American hierarchy a central institution for the intellectual formation of both clergy and laity. The letter remains a key reference point for Catholic higher education in America and for the relationship between the Holy See and local episcopal conferences in founding academic institutions.
