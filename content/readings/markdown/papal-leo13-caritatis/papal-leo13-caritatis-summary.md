# Caritatis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XIII encourages Polish Catholics to preserve their inherited faith through fidelity to the Church, strong family life, religious education, and a well-formed clergy. He links stable public life to just government and conscientious citizenship, urging obedience to civil authority while defending the Church’s freedom to carry out its mission. Schools should unite intellectual development with Christian formation, and seminaries should combine sound study with holiness and pastoral competence. Renewing the teaching of Rerum novarum, he commends workers’ associations, mutual aid, and other charitable institutions.

The encyclical then addresses Catholics under different governments. For those under Russian rule, Leo recalls agreements concerning seminaries and ecclesiastical education and urges their protection. For those under Habsburg rule, he supports Catholic scholarship, religious orders, and fraternal relations with Ruthenians, including respect for their legitimate rites. He also encourages the faithful of Gniezno and Poznan. Throughout, he presents pastoral cooperation and lawful public conduct as means of safeguarding Catholic life amid national and political divisions.

## Key themes

- Marriage, religious education, and continuing clerical formation
- Workers’ associations and practical Christian charity
- Catholic rights under different governments and respect for Ruthenian rites
