## Summary

*Nos Es Muy Conocida* is a 1937 encyclical by Pope Pius XI addressing the persecution of the Catholic Church in Mexico. Written to the Mexican bishops, it offers encouragement to the faithful enduring state-imposed restrictions, calls for priestly holiness and the formation of lay apostolates through Catholic Action, and outlines a program for the moral, social, and religious reconstruction of Mexican society.

## Historical Context

The encyclical was issued during the aftermath of Mexico's Cristero War (1926–1929), a violent uprising by Catholic peasants against the anticlerical and secularizing policies of the Mexican government. Although the war had formally ended, the government continued suppressing religious practice — closing seminaries, restricting clergy, and promoting secular education. Pius XI had already addressed the situation in his 1926 apostolic letter *Paterna Sane Solicitudo*. By 1937, with tensions still high and many Catholics forced into exile or clandestine worship, the Pope sought to rally the Church and articulate a strategy of spiritual renewal rather than armed resistance.

## Key Themes

- **Priestly holiness as the foundation**: The renewal of Mexico depends first on priests formed in virtue and doctrine, especially through seminaries abroad when local ones are impossible.
- **The indispensable role of the laity**: Catholic Action — the organized apostolate of laypeople under hierarchical guidance — is essential for reaching a scattered and persecuted flock.
- **Formation over numbers**: Quality of collaborators matters more than quantity; leaders must be carefully selected and supernaturally formed.
- **Social justice as evangelization**: The Church must work for the material and religious elevation of workers, peasants, and Indigenous peoples, applying principles of commutative and social justice without endorsing violent revolution.
- **Duty toward youth, students, and emigrants**: Special attention must be given to children (keeping them from secular schools), university students (future leaders), and Mexican emigrants (vulnerable to apostasy abroad).
- **Legitimate defense, not violence**: The Pope lays out principles for when and how Catholics may defend their rights, while strictly forbidding clergy and Catholic Action from engaging in partisan politics or armed insurrection.
- **Unity, obedience, and discipline**: He repeatedly calls for cohesion under the bishops, warning that internal dissent destroys the effectiveness of the apostolate.

## Significance

*Nos Es Muy Conocida* is a key document in the Church's response to modern state persecution and a landmark papal endorsement of Catholic Action as the primary vehicle for lay participation in the Church's mission. It demonstrates Pius XI's consistent teaching — also found in *Ubi Arcano Dei* (1922), *Quadragesimo Anno* (1931), and *Ad Catholici Sacerdotii* (1935) — that spiritual renewal, social justice, and lay organization are inseparable. The encyclical also reflects the Church's difficult balancing act in Mexico: condemning anticlerical tyranny while rejecting violent revolution, and advocating for the poor without endorsing socialist solutions. Its themes of lay apostolate and religious liberty remain influential in modern Catholic social teaching.
