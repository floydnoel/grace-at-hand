# Pastorale officium · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI appoints Theodore Abukarim as apostolic delegate and visitor to the people of Abyssinia. He explains that sending a bishop of the Coptic rite is intended to promote Catholic faith and communion with Rome among a population whose worship he identifies with that rite.

The pope commends Abukarim's religious zeal, prudence, learning, and integrity, and notes his existing pastoral care of Coptic Catholics in Egypt as relevant experience. Acting on the advice of Propaganda Fide, he grants the rights and faculties belonging to the new commission while preserving that congregation's authority. The brief is an appointment to a specific pastoral mission, without describing the results of the visitation.

## Key themes

- A Coptic bishop commissioned for Abyssinia
- Communion with Rome as the mission’s stated aim
- Experience in Egypt and delegated pastoral faculties
