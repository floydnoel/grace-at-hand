# Augustissimam beatissimi · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI appeals to the bishops of the Catholic world for renewed financial support to finish rebuilding the basilica of Saint Paul after its destruction by fire. He recalls Leo XII's initial commitment of papal funds and worldwide appeal, the continuation under Pius VIII, and his own efforts despite depleted revenues and declining donations. He reports the recent inauguration of the completed transept and consecration of the central altar above Paul's tomb, while stressing that substantial work remains.

The appeal grounds material generosity in gratitude for Paul's preaching, sufferings, writings, and martyrdom. Drawing on John Chrysostom's praise of the apostle and the biblical contributions for the tabernacle, Gregory presents the rebuilding as a shared act of worship. Bishops are asked to encourage offerings according to the faithful's means, collect them, and send them to Rome so that the restored basilica can be completed.

## Key themes

- Devotion to Paul as the motive for rebuilding his basilica
- Progress of reconstruction and continuing financial need
- Worldwide collections coordinated by the bishops
