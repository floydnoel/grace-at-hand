# Inter multiplices · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VIII transfers Cape Breton from the Diocese of Quebec to the apostolic vicariate of Nova Scotia. After consulting the cardinals of Propaganda Fide, he judges that the neighboring vicariate can administer the territory's religious affairs more readily and assist the spread of Catholic faith there.

The brief formally separates Cape Breton from Quebec, joins it to Nova Scotia's vicariate, and subjects it to the jurisdiction of the apostolic vicar holding that office. Contrary provisions, including obligations associated with Quebec, do not prevent the transfer. The decision is framed as an exercise of papal responsibility to adapt ecclesiastical boundaries to the faithful's pastoral needs.

## Key themes

- Transfer of Cape Breton's ecclesiastical jurisdiction
- Geographic proximity and practical pastoral administration
