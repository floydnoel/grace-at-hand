# Ex debito pastoralis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI establishes an apostolic vicariate for Korea in anticipation of missionaries being able to enter the kingdom. He identifies two practical reasons for the arrangement: the need to provide catechesis and sacraments for Christians there, and the rarity and difficulty of communication with neighboring ecclesiastical regions.

Following the advice of Propaganda Fide, the pope makes the new vicar completely independent of the bishop of Beijing. The Holy See will choose the officeholder and grant the faculties customary for vicars in China and nearby regions. The brief gives the territorial and jurisdictional decision enduring legal force, without naming the vicar in this document.

## Key themes

- Missionary access and the needs of Korean Christians
- An independent jurisdiction separated from Beijing
- Appointment and faculties reserved to the Holy See
