# Ubi prima · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX condemns the election of a rival Armenian Catholic patriarch following continued resistance to his delegate and refusal to sign a prescribed profession of faith. He recounts the declaration of schism and major excommunication against the opponents, then their assembly with four bishops to elect Giacomo Batiaziarian as James Peter IX in place of Anthony Peter IX Hassun.

The pope declares the assembly and attempted election unlawful and void, denies the elected claimant jurisdiction, and prohibits him from using the patriarchal title or exercising episcopal ministry. He confirms Hassun as the sole legitimate patriarch of Cilicia and directs Armenian Catholics to remain in communion with him and the Apostolic See. Pius praises the clergy and faithful who resisted division and calls the separated bishops and their followers to return. The letter presents the Roman See as both judge of the dispute and a father ready to receive repentant members.

## Key themes

- Rejected profession of faith and the declaration of schism
- Invalid rival election and confirmation of the recognized patriarch
- Ecclesial obedience and an invitation to reconciliation
