# Etsi Iam Diu · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII writes particularly to the bishops of America about civil disorder and its consequences for religious life. He deplores publications attacking ecclesiastical and civil authority and associations he regards as destructive, arguing that factional conflict undermines the common obligations of rulers and subjects and endangers the integrity of religion.

The letter calls on bishops to speak actively for concord and assist distressed populations. Its political direction is explicit: Leo praises Ferdinand, king of Spain, and urges the bishops to place before their flocks the example of Spaniards in Europe who sacrificed for religion and legitimate authority. He also asks them to relieve anxiety and pray for the end of discord. Throughout, the pope connects the preservation of Catholic life with restoration of public tranquility, expressing paternal concern for communities far from Rome.

## Key themes

- Religious dangers attributed to civil division and hostile publications
- Papal support for the Spanish king and legitimate authority
- Pastoral assistance, public exhortation, and prayer for peace
