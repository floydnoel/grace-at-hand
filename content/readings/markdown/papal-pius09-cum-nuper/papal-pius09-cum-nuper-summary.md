## Summary

*Cum Nuper* (1858) is an encyclical by Pope Pius IX addressed to the bishops of the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, written in response to a letter he received from them at Christmas. The document expresses the pope's sorrow over devastating earthquakes that killed countless people in the region, and urges bishops to prioritize clerical reform — rooting out corruption, ensuring only worthy candidates are ordained, improving clerical education, and enforcing canonical discipline — as the primary remedy for divine scourges provoked by sin.

## Historical Context

The encyclical was written in January 1858, in the penultimate year of Pius IX's long pontificate (1846–1878). The Kingdom of the Two Sicilies had recently suffered catastrophic earthquakes (December 1857), which the pope interprets biblically as punishment for sin. The wider context includes the Church's struggle against liberal nationalism (the Risorgimento), which would soon sweep away the Papal States; Pius IX warns repeatedly of "diabolical errors," "perverse doctrine," and "unwholesome books" that threaten Christian society. King Ferdinand II, an absolutist ally of the papacy, is praised for his support.

## Key Themes

- **Clerical reform and discipline**: The pope's central concern is that clergy have "forgotten their vocation" and scandalize the faithful; bishops must purge abuses, train younger clergy in piety and learning, and ordain only after rigorous scrutiny.
- **Catechesis and Catholic education**: Bishops must ensure young people of both sexes receive a sound Catholic education in all schools, public and private, to combat the "manifold evils" of the age.
- **Combatting bad books**: A strong emphasis on censorship — bishops must inspect all books on philosophy, theology, and canon/civil law to protect the faithful from error.
- **Episcopal governance**: Bishops must uphold Church rights, administer goods faithfully, handle curial business with honesty, and enforce ecclesiastical sentences with civil assistance when needed.
- **Pastoral care for religious and nuns**: Religious are to be helpers to bishops; nuns need special attention, including appropriate confessors per Benedict XIV's constitution.

## Significance

*Cum Nuper* is a pastoral encyclical focused on internal Church renewal rather than doctrinal development, reflecting Pius IX's broader concern for centralizing episcopal authority and enforcing clerical discipline in the face of secularizing trends. While less famous than his later *Quanta Cura* (1864) with its *Syllabus of Errors*, it exemplifies the same combative, restorationist mindset — the Church as a fortress besieged by modern errors, needing purified clergy and vigilant oversight to survive.
