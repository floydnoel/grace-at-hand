# Quo luctu · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VI responds to French bishops who report that the revolutionary upheaval prevents the public celebration of worship according to ordinary ecclesiastical rules. To help them provide for their dioceses, he grants temporary exceptions while necessity and the Church's welfare require them, subject to the pleasure of the Apostolic See.

The brief permits consecration of the holy oils outside Holy Thursday and with the priests who can be assembled. It relaxes the existing timetable for minor and major orders, while requiring separate, nonconsecutive days for subdiaconate, diaconate, and priesthood. It also allows priests to bless vestments and tabernacles, consecrate chalices and patens using properly consecrated chrism, and reconcile desecrated churches. Bishops receive broad authority to subdelegate within these provisions. The document treats flexibility in historical ceremonial discipline as a means of preserving legitimate ministry amid exceptional disruption.

## Key themes

- Episcopal responsibility when ordinary worship is obstructed
- Temporary exceptions to historical ordination discipline
- Delegation of blessings and reconciliation of churches
