# Inter multiplices · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VIII converts the apostolic vicariate covering Alabama and Florida into a diocese with its episcopal seat at Mobile. He recalls Leo XII's creation of the vicariate in 1825 as an interim arrangement pending a permanent diocese. Bishop Michael Portior's visit to Rome and detailed account of local religious conditions have supplied the information needed for the change.

Following consultation with the cardinals of Propaganda Fide, the pope gives the new diocese the boundaries of Alabama and Florida and the customary diocesan rights. It is placed within the metropolitan jurisdiction of the archbishop of Baltimore. The brief directs those responsible to execute the arrangement and treats the establishment as a measure for strengthening Catholic life and serving the faithful more effectively.

## Key themes

- Transition from an apostolic vicariate to a diocese
- Mobile as episcopal seat for Alabama and Florida
- Metropolitan relationship with Baltimore
