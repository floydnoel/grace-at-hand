# Libentissime fraternitas · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI commends an initiative in Bruges to help poor couples living outside marriage prepare for and enter sacramental marriage. At the bishop's request, he authorizes indulgences comparable to those previously granted for a similar work in Mechelen, leaving their administration to the bishop and his successors.

The grants recognize the work's devotional and practical activities: meetings, religious instruction, preparing couples for marriage, attending their weddings, and assisting with baptism where needed. The letter also provides an annual plenary indulgence under sacramental and prayer conditions and one at the approach of death under specified conditions. It permits application of the indulgences to the faithful departed. Gregory presents the initiative as a means of restoring Christian life through instruction, sacramental preparation, and sustained personal assistance.

## Key themes

- Practical and catechetical preparation for poor couples’ marriages
- Historical indulgences for supporters and participants
- Sacramental life and prayer for the departed
