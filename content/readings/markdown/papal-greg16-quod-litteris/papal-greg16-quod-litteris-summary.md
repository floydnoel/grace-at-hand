# Quod litteris · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI tells the bishop of Rennes that a new letter from Lamennais has disappointed the hopes raised by his earlier request for a clear formula of obedience. The pope judges its expressed intentions inconsistent with the unambiguous submission required and explains that Cardinal Bartolomeo of Ostia has already communicated his displeasure.

Despite this renewed disappointment, Gregory insists that his paternal affection remains open to Lamennais and that he still desires a sincere resolution. He confirms the bishop's role as intermediary and calls upon bishops generally to spread the teaching of the encyclical. The letter connects reconciliation with the wider pastoral responsibility to preserve unity, remove scandal, and prevent error from gaining followers, ending with prayer for divine help rather than announcing a settled outcome.

## Key themes

- Insistence on an unambiguous response to papal teaching
- Continuing hope for personal reconciliation
- Episcopal dissemination of the encyclical
