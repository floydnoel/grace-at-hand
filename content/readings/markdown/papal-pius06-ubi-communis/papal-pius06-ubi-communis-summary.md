# Ubi communis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VI extends his renewed warnings against the Civil Constitution of the Clergy to Corsica, sending the legitimate bishops copies for circulation among clergy and people. He recounts the election and unauthorized consecration of Ignazio Francesco Guasco as a bishop for the whole island despite the continued existence of its lawful bishops. He rejects Guasco's asserted jurisdiction and the pastoral appointments and matrimonial dispensations made under it, while noting his suspension from exercising episcopal orders.

The pope contrasts these developments with the fidelity of Corsican bishops, clergy, and laity who have resisted the new system, sometimes through imprisonment or exile. He encourages their perseverance and warns that continued obstinacy will lead to the final canonical penalty after the announced periods of admonition. At the same time, he grants the Corsican bishops the emergency faculties already available to the French hierarchy, allowing them to provide more effectively for their people during the same crisis.

## Key themes

- Application of the French ecclesiastical crisis to Corsica
- Rejection of Guasco's claimed jurisdiction
- Fidelity under imprisonment and episcopal exile
- Extension of emergency pastoral faculties
