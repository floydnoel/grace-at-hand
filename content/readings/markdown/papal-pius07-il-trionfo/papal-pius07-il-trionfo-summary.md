# Il trionfo · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VII announces his liberation after nearly five years of forced absence and captivity. He thanks divine providence and acknowledges the allied powers' efforts, recalling his grief at being unable to assist either the wider Church or his own subjects during imprisonment. Preparing to return to Rome, he sends a delegate to resume temporal government there and in the provinces with a state commission and subordinate delegates.

The pope links temporal sovereignty to the independence of his spiritual office and reserves his claims to Church territories not yet restored. He also insists on public tranquility during the transition. Only sovereign authority, he declares, may investigate alleged wrongdoing, determine guilt, and impose proportionate punishment; subjects must not take punitive power over one another. The letter thus combines thanksgiving and plans for provisional administration with a warning against private reprisals and disturbances as papal government resumes.

## Key themes

- Thanksgiving for release after prolonged captivity
- Provisional restoration of papal temporal government
- Reservation of territorial claims
- Public order and the rejection of private reprisals
