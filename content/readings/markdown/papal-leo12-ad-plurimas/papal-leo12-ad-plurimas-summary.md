# Ad Plurimas · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII appeals to the bishops of the Catholic world for help rebuilding the basilica of Saint Paul after a devastating fire. He recounts attempts to preserve the surviving fabric and his abandoned hope of using the church during the forthcoming jubilee. Further discoveries of structural damage made ceremonies unsafe and complete rebuilding necessary, while the papal state's reduced resources could not meet the expense alone.

The pope grounds his appeal in gratitude for Paul's missionary labor, sufferings, writings, and martyrdom, and in the longstanding veneration of his tomb, which survived the destruction. He calls on bishops to awaken this devotion and organize contributions, including modest gifts according to the faithful's means. Collections are to be sent to Rome so that the basilica may rise again with the splendor the available offerings permit. The work is presented as a common act of honor toward the apostle of the nations.

## Key themes

- Unsafe ruins and the decision to rebuild the basilica
- Gratitude for Paul’s ministry and veneration of his surviving tomb
- Worldwide episcopal collections and offerings of every size
