# Pastorale Officium · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI appoints the English Benedictine priest Giovanni Poulden as apostolic vicar of the Madras mission, referring to a separate letter naming him titular bishop of Hierocaesarea. The pope presents episcopal leadership as a means of strengthening religious care in the mission's established territory and praises the appointee's piety, learning, prudence, and zeal.

The appointment confers complete spiritual jurisdiction and places the mission's temporal rights, possessions, hospices, and churches previously belonging to the Capuchin mission under the new vicar's authority, in the name of Propaganda Fide and subject to its authority. Those concerned are ordered to receive and obey him. The brief also supports the lawful measures he may need to enforce his administration.

## Key themes

- Episcopal leadership for the Madras mission
- Transfer of spiritual and temporal administration
- Continuing authority of Propaganda Fide
