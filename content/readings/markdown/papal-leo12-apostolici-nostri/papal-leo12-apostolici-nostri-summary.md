# Apostolici Nostri · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII appoints Mariano Talavera, bishop-elect of Tricala, as apostolic vicar of the episcopal Church of Guiana. The pope explains that the see has lacked a pastor for many years, causing serious harm to Catholics in its distant and difficult territory.

The appointment entrusts Talavera with spiritual and temporal government until the Holy See provides another arrangement, excluding others from exercising ordinary jurisdiction there. It grants the authority needed for ordinary and delegated administration in the pope's name and, after episcopal consecration, functions requiring episcopal orders. The clergy and faithful are instructed to recognize his authority and obey his lawful pastoral directions.

## Key themes

- Remedy for a prolonged episcopal vacancy
- Interim spiritual and temporal administration
- Recognition of the appointed vicar by clergy and faithful
