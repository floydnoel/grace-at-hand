## Summary

*Ingravescentibus Malis* (1937) is Pope Pius XI's encyclical on the Holy Rosary, written to urge the faithful to pray the Rosary as a remedy against the mounting evils of the modern world. The Pope presents the Rosary as a powerful spiritual weapon against communism, secularism, and the erosion of Christian faith and morals, calling for a return to Christ through the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary.

## Historical Context

The encyclical was issued in September 1937, at a time of grave global tension. The Spanish Civil War was raging; Stalin's Soviet Union was actively promoting worldwide Communist revolution; Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy had consolidated power and were suppressing the Church. Earlier that year, Pius XI had issued *Divini Redemptoris* condemning Communism, and *Mit brennender Sorge* condemning Nazism. The encyclical also references a public sacrilege against Mary in Poland (then under repressive rule). Pius XI had recently recovered from a serious illness, which he attributed to the intercession of St. Thérèse of Lisieux and the Virgin Mary.

## Key Themes

- **The Rosary as a universal remedy** — Pius XI presents the Rosary as accessible to all, from the simplest faithful to kings and scholars, and as a powerful weapon against heresy and sin.
- **Mary as mediatrix** — Following St. Bernard, the Pope teaches that God wills us to receive all graces through Mary; the Rosary is the privileged means of seeking her intercession.
- **Anti-Communism** — The encyclical explicitly links the Rosary to the defeat of Communism, drawing a parallel with St. Dominic's victory over the Albigenses.
- **Contemplation of the mysteries** — The Rosary is not mere repetition but a form of prayer that places the joys, sorrows, and glories of Christ and Mary before the eyes of the faithful, nourishing faith, hope, and charity.
- **Family prayer** — Pius XI especially exhorts families to pray the Rosary together daily, presenting it as a source of domestic peace and heavenly gifts.
- **Reparation for blasphemy** — The Pope uses the occasion to denounce a public insult to Mary in Poland and calls for acts of reparation.

## Significance

*Ingravescentibus Malis* is one of the most important modern papal documents on Marian devotion. It reaffirms the Rosary's central place in Catholic piety at a moment of acute crisis for the Church and Western civilization. Together with Leo XIII's Rosary encyclicals and later documents like Paul VI's *Marialis Cultus* and John Paul II's *Rosarium Virginis Mariae*, it forms part of the Church's enduring magisterial teaching on the Rosary. Its explicit linkage of the Rosary to the struggle against totalitarianism — both Communist and pagan-statist — gives it a distinctive historical and political as well as spiritual importance.
