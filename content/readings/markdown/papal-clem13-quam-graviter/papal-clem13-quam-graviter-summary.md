# Quam graviter · English editorial summary

## Summary

Clement XIII protests three recent French royal decrees, arguing that they subject the Church’s teaching and disciplinary authority to secular approval. He asks how the limits of spiritual and temporal power can survive if civil officials may annul ecclesiastical decisions concerning faith, discipline, or conduct. He also objects to secular revision of religious orders’ constitutions without consulting the Holy See.

Addressing the bishops assembled in Paris, he urges them to explain these consequences to the king and seek relief. Clement expresses confidence in the king’s religious intentions and says he is writing personally to obtain a favorable hearing for their representations.

## Key themes

- Independence of ecclesiastical teaching and discipline
- Religious orders and consultation with the Holy See
- Bishops’ representations to the French king
