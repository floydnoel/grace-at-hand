# Ecclesia Dei · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX rejects the allegation that the Society of Jesus controls his decisions or determines his opposition to the government that has taken the Papal States. He explains that, after seeking divine guidance, he consults people of different ranks and religious institutes according to their competence. His reliance on Jesuits for particular tasks and his esteem for their service do not, he insists, amount to subjection to them. He sees the accusation as preparation for suppressing religious communities.

The letter then criticizes proposed governmental guarantees for papal sovereignty. Pius argues that concessions granted by another power place their recipient under the grantor’s authority and discretion, thereby undermining the independence they profess to secure. He interprets these arrangements through the Church’s conformity to the suffering Christ and expresses confidence in its eventual vindication. Addressed to Cardinal Patrizi, the letter combines defense of a religious order with rejection of a political settlement.

## Key themes

- Papal consultation distinguished from alleged Jesuit control
- Defense of religious communities against suppression
- Rejection of guarantees dependent on another sovereign’s discretion
