# Ubi primum · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius IX asks the bishops of the Catholic world for their judgment on defining the Immaculate Conception of Mary. He recalls petitions from bishops, chapters, religious communities, and other Catholics, together with permissions already granted for liturgical use of the title Immaculate. Expressing his own longstanding Marian devotion, he explains that theologians and cardinals have been commissioned to examine the question carefully.

The encyclical requests public prayers for guidance and prompt reports on devotion among clergy and people, their desire for a definition, and, especially, each bishop’s own opinion and wishes. It also permits bishops to authorize the special office of Mary’s conception already used by the Roman clergy, without a further application to Rome. The letter thus conducts consultation and encourages prayer and worship in preparation for a decision; it does not itself issue the later dogmatic definition.

## Key themes

- Worldwide episcopal consultation on a possible definition
- Theological examination joined to public prayer
- Permission for a special liturgical office
