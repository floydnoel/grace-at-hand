# Ecclesiarum Omnium · English editorial summary

## Summary

Leo XII extends an existing arrangement for governing Goa during an episcopal vacancy. Gregory XIII had empowered the bishop of Cochin to administer the metropolitan see while it awaited a new pastor, a practical response to the long and difficult journey from Rome and the dangers of an extended absence of episcopal leadership.

At the Portuguese regent's request, Leo provides substitutes if Cochin is also vacant or its bishop dies while administering Goa. The archbishop of Cranganore is to assume the same powers; if that see too lacks a bishop, the bishop of Mylapore is authorized to govern Goa. The brief preserves the applicable conditions of the earlier grant while establishing a clearer sequence of interim administrators.

## Key themes

- Continuity of government during a distant see’s vacancy
- Successive provision through Cochin, Cranganore, and Mylapore
- Preservation of conditions attached to the earlier papal grant
