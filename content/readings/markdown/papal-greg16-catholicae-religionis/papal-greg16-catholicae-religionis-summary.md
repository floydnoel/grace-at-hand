# Catholicae religionis · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI calls the universal Church to pray for Spain while renewing his condemnation of government measures against ecclesiastical authority. Recalling his earlier public protests, he describes confiscations, mistreatment of clergy, and a proposed law that would subject the Church and its property to civil supremacy. He objects especially to restrictions on communication with Rome, papal rescripts, religious appeals, dispensations, and papal confirmation of bishops, including penalties for seeking such confirmation.

The pope declares these measures without ecclesiastical force and urges bishops everywhere to enlist their clergy and people in prayer. He specifically directs public supplications in the Papal States and grants a jubilee-form plenary indulgence under stated sacramental and prayer conditions. Invoking Mary, Peter, and the saints associated with Spain, he frames common prayer as an expression of the unity of Catholics and asks for the restoration of the Spanish Church's peace and freedom.

## Key themes

- Civil restrictions on relations with the Holy See
- Papal confirmation of bishops and ecclesiastical jurisdiction
- Worldwide prayer and a historical jubilee indulgence
