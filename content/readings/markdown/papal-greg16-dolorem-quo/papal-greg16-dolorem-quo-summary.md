# Dolorem quo · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI reproves the archbishop of Freiburg after reports that numerous Catholic clergy joined the inauguration procession of a Protestant church. He distinguishes attendance at a civil occasion from participation honoring non-Catholic worship and argues that the latter creates scandal by appearing to approve religious error. He calls for a clear correction while affirming his personal goodwill toward the archbishop.

The letter also addresses marriage discipline, liturgical practice, catechesis, and clerical education. Gregory announces faculties concerning marriages within prohibited degrees of kinship, partly in hope of reducing mixed marriages, while insisting on the safeguards then required for Catholic religious practice and children's education. He resists proposals for an altered ritual book, recommends the Roman Catechism, and urges sound teaching in schools and seminaries. Finally, he requests a prompt account of local ecclesiastical conditions and compliance with earlier papal directions.

## Key themes

- Clerical participation in a Protestant church inauguration
- Historical marriage dispensations and religious safeguards
- Approved ritual, catechetical teaching, and priestly formation
