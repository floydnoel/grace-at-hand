# Inter Graves · English editorial summary

## Summary

Welcoming the bishops’ synod at Lima, Leo XIII encourages further meetings so that common counsel can strengthen Catholic faith and pastoral life in Peru. He identifies four priorities. Seminaries must unite moral formation with serious learning, particularly Thomistic philosophy, biblical studies, and sufficient knowledge of the physical sciences to answer objections to religion. Parish priests must be chosen for holiness, knowledge, selfless concern for souls, and readiness to serve rather than financial or personal advantage.

Leo also calls for more missionaries to Indigenous peoples, linking evangelization with the cultural changes he considers beneficial. Finally, he recommends Catholic newspapers and periodicals as instruments of instruction and defense of the faith. Their writers should combine firm principles with moderation, prudence, charity, respect for civil authority, and obedience to episcopal guidance. These practical concerns give the bishops an agenda for future synods and connect pastoral renewal with both education and public communication.

## Key themes

- Episcopal synods and cooperation in pastoral planning
- Seminary learning and the careful selection of parish priests
- Indigenous missions and responsible Catholic journalism
