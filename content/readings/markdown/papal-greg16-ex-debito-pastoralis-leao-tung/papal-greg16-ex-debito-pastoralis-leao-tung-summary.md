# Ex debito pastoralis Leao Tung · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI creates an apostolic vicariate centered on Liaodong after receiving reports that Christian life in parts of the Beijing diocese is in serious danger through inadequate pastoral support. He explains that the bishop of Beijing cannot give the affected territories the necessary attention and that preventive action is needed to preserve the faith.

The brief detaches Liaodong from Beijing, modifying the earlier settlement of its diocesan boundaries and ending delegated authority over the relevant territories. A bishop and apostolic vicar appointed by, and directly dependent on, the Holy See will govern the new jurisdiction. His authority also extends to the parts of Tartary, Mongolia, and Manchuria formerly entrusted to Beijing that have not already been assigned to another apostolic vicar.

## Key themes

- Pastoral danger in remote territories of the Beijing diocese
- Creation of a vicariate directly dependent on Rome
- Jurisdiction over additional unassigned northern territories
