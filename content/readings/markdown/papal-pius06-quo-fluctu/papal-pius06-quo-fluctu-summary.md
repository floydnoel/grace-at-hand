# Quo fluctu · English editorial summary

## Summary

Pius VI grants extraordinary faculties to French bishops after learning that public worship and the usual observance of ecclesiastical ceremonies have become impossible. The concessions are tied to the continuing emergency, the Church's needs, and the judgment of bishops remaining in communion with the Apostolic See.

The bishops may consecrate the holy oils with whatever priests are available and outside Holy Thursday. They may confer the four minor orders together or on separate days and advance candidates through subdiaconate, diaconate, and priesthood on nonconsecutive Sundays or feast days without the ordinary schedules and prescribed intervals. The brief also authorizes priests to bless necessary liturgical objects, consecrate chalices and patens with chrism from a Catholic bishop in communion with Rome, and reconcile profaned churches under the stated conditions. These historical exceptions aim to sustain sacramental ministry when normal discipline cannot be followed.

## Key themes

- Extraordinary sacramental provision during persecution
- Relaxation of historical ordination schedules and intervals
- Consecration of oils and sacred vessels
- Restoration of profaned churches to worship
