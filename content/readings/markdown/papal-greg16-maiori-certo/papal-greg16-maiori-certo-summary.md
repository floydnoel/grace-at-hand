# Maiori certo · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI approves the Belgian bishops' proposal for a Catholic university under their direction. He praises their pastoral zeal and their decision to seek apostolic authorization, explaining that the pope's responsibility for safeguarding faith includes oversight of sacred learning publicly taught in universities.

The brief commends a form of education grounded in charity and the fear of God, rather than intellectual pride. Gregory expresses confidence that the bishops' care will guide students toward this purpose and offers the project his protection. He also reminds them that the university must be established consistently with the Council of Trent's provisions assigning bishops responsibility for clerical formation in diocesan seminaries. His approval therefore combines encouragement for the new institution with preservation of existing episcopal educational duties.

## Key themes

- Papal approval of a university directed by the Belgian bishops
- Learning ordered to charity and religious formation
- Continued responsibility for diocesan seminaries
