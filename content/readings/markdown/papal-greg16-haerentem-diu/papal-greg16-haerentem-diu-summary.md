# Haerentem diu · English editorial summary

## Summary

Gregory XVI explains why he has ordered a detailed account of his efforts on behalf of Catholics in the Russian Empire to be circulated among the cardinals. His principal concern is that his interventions have not become known in the affected territories, allowing a rumor to spread that he has remained silent or abandoned the Catholic cause.

The pope rejects that charge and insists that he has sought remedies from the beginning of his pontificate. The accompanying exposition is intended to show the faithful that he has fulfilled his apostolic duty. Despite continuing distress, he expresses hope that the emperor of Russia, also king of Poland, will respond favorably to Catholic petitions, and he calls for confident common prayer.

## Key themes

- Rumors caused by the concealment of papal interventions
- Publication of a record of advocacy for Russian Catholics
- Hope for imperial redress and common prayer
