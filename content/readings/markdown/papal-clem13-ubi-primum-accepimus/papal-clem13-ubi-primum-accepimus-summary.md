# Ubi primum accepimus · English editorial summary

## Summary

Clement XIII welcomes the imperial electors’ assembly at Frankfurt and urges the election of Archduke Joseph of Austria as King of the Romans. He praises Joseph’s character, upbringing, and reported devotion to religion, expressing hope that his election will strengthen both the empire and the Catholic Church.

The pope asks the electors to use their deliberations to protect Christian religion, the faith, and ecclesiastical rights. He introduces Nicholas, archbishop of Trajanopolis, as his nuncio to the gathering and requests that they receive him honorably and trust his representations as those of the pope himself.

## Key themes

- Papal support for Archduke Joseph’s election
- Protection of religion and ecclesiastical rights
- Reception and authority of the papal nuncio
